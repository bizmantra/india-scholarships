# Canada study permit facts for Indian students (master's), official sources only

All pages were read on **2026-11-28** (28 November 2026). www.canada.ca does not answer the box's own network, so those pages were read through a fetch tool that returns the live page text. `ircc.canada.ca`, `bankofcanada.ca` and the university pages were read with a normal browser user agent. No agent, coaching, blog or news-site figure is used. "NOT PUBLISHED" means no official page I could read gives it.

Sources: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (`canada.ca`, `ircc.canada.ca`), EduCanada (`educanada.ca`, Global Affairs Canada), Gouvernement du Québec (`quebec.ca`), Bank of Canada (`bankofcanada.ca`).
