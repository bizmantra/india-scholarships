## Other facts worth knowing (all from the notification)
- The student's parent must be a CM-KISAN beneficiary (small or marginal farmer, or landless agricultural household), checked by the Chief District Agriculture Officer (4(i), 8).
- One scholarship only, and for one professional course only (4(iv), 4(v)). A student on another scholarship can switch to this one.
- PhD: only courses related to the eligible PG courses, in government institutions inside the State, up to 4 years; PG up to 2 years (section 3).
- Applications are online only, at https://scholarship.odisha.gov.in; paid by DBT to an Aadhaar-linked account in the student's own name (5(vii), 6).
- Annexure-1 has 41 private-institute courses with a yearly amount each, and says the scholarship is paid as the actual fee, limited to that amount.
