All pages read on **2026-11-28**. No agent, blog, ranking-site or news figure is used. Quotes are exact.

How the Melbourne course pages were read: the pages build their tabs (Fees, Entry requirements, How to apply) in the browser, so a plain read shows only the top box. I downloaded each page's HTML and read the course data the page itself embeds (the same data the tabs show). Where the quote below is from the visible top box, it says "visible". Where it comes from the embedded course data, it says "page data". The Melbourne pages themselves show "Last updated" 2026-11-28 10:02 (+1100) for Master of Information Technology and 2026-11-28 10:01 for Master of Data Science (page data).

Not used on purpose: any ranking or "top" claim (the university pages carry "#1" and "#22" lines; ranking claims are not figures we can check, and the rules ban them), any agent or blog number.

## Conflicts and gaps (left off the pages)

- OSHC price: no official figure read. Totals say "before health cover".
- Second-year fee: the pages give the first-year fee and the full-course total only. The second-year fee is not stated, so none is given.
- Fees for the 1-year and 1.5-year Master of Information Technology options: not on the page. Not given.
- The page labels the Master of Information Technology start-year date "February intake" and lists the start as "March, July". Both are quoted as written.
- Melbourne shows 2026 fees in the top box and 2027 fees in the fee data. The pages use 2027 (the year a student starting in 2027 pays) and say so.
- Indian marks needed for "65% WAM or equivalent": Melbourne publishes a calculator, not a table. No Indian percentage is given on the pages.
- Rent in Melbourne: not repeated here; the cost-of-living guide carries the calculator figures.
- Only one university is covered. No other university page was brought up to the same standard in this pass (reasons in the report).
- Scholarship record fields not on the page: GPA cut-off, number of awards for India, any India-only deadline. Left blank.
