## Exchange rate (INR)

No official KRW to INR reference rate exists. The RBI reference rates on its home page cover USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, AED, IDR and RUB, not KRW. I used an official cross rate built from two dated official rates:

| Input | Value | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| RBI reference rate | INR 92.9139 per USD (page text: "INR / 1 USD", as at 1.00 pm of November 27, 2026) | https://www.rbi.org.in/ | read 2026-11-28 |
| Bank of Korea won per dollar close | KRW 1,337.9 per USD, 2026-11-27 (ECOS table 731Y003, item 0000003; public sample endpoint) | https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticSearch/sample/json/kr/1/10/731Y003/D/20261127/20261127/0000003 | read 2026-11-28 |

Result: INR 0.069448 per KRW (92.9139 / 1,337.9). The pages round it to **INR 0.0694 per won** in text and compute rupee values from the unrounded figure. Examples: KRW 20,000,000 = INR 13.89 lakh; KRW 16,000,000 = INR 11.11 lakh; KRW 750,000 = INR 52,086; KRW 1,000,000 = INR 69,448; USD 60 = INR 5,575 (RBI rate directly); USD 16,000 = INR 14.87 lakh; USD 30,000 = INR 27.87 lakh.

**Owner decision (hardcoded rates).** The rupee figures sit as dated text in the guides. `lib/study-abroad/rbi-rate.ts` reads USD, GBP and EUR only. To avoid a stale rate in the Korea pages, add a live KRW read (for example the RBI USD rate divided by a live won per dollar rate from the Bank of Korea) and have the pages render rupee values from code. This PR does not change `rbi-rate.ts`.
