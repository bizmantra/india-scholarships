## Notes and conflicts

1. Every fee figure on these pages carries the page's own caveat: "information on this page is for 2026-27 entry and should be used as guidance for 2027-28 entry". The 2027-28 view shows the figures above.
2. The international deadline table (apply page) lists intakes from Nov 2026 to Jul 2027. It does not list the September 2027 intake, even though the course pages list September 2027 as a start date. This is why the September deadline is NOT CONFIRMED.
3. The deposit rule for Indian students is only partly stated. Punjab, Uttarakhand and Haryana pay full first-year tuition. Other Indian states are not named, so the GBP 8,000 "all other countries" line is an inference. Confirm before publishing.
4. The related-course list has only two links. Other Coventry names from the same faculty were not checked, so this list is incomplete.
5. The DS and MBA IELTS quotes were re-read on 2026-11-03 from the 2027-28 views.
