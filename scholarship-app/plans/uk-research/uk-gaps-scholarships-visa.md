Research date: 2026-11-02. Official sources only (britishcouncil.org / britishcouncil.in, chevening.org, gov.uk, ukcisa.org.uk, ac.uk).
Rules applied: every fact has an exact figure or date, a quote under 15 words, a URL and the date read. Deadlines are not inferred. Where a date is not published it says NOT CONFIRMED and gives the last known cycle with its year. Secondary listings were not used for any number or date.

Status key: VERIFIED = read on the official page on the date shown. NOT CONFIRMED = not published on the official page read. EXCLUDED = found only in a secondary listing or on a page that could not be read.
