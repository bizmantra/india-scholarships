## 1. Chevening (UK Government, India)

- Official page: https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/india/ (read 2026-11-01)
- Award value: NOT CONFIRMED. Page says "Chevening Scholarships are fully-funded." (https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/, read 2026-11-01). No itemised breakdown on the India page.
- Eligibility for Indians (https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/india/eligibility/, read 2026-11-01):
  - "Have at least two years' work experience"
  - "Hold an undergraduate degree that qualifies you for a UK master's programme."
  - "Commit to returning to your home country" for at least two years after the scholarship ends.
- Application dates, next cycle: NOT CONFIRMED. The India page says "Applications for 2027-2028 Chevening Scholarships are now closed."
- How to apply: online application through the Chevening portal; check eligibility, write the application, keep the unique access code for each stage (India page).
- Secondary (not used for any figure): none read.

## 2. Commonwealth Master's Scholarship (Commonwealth Scholarship Commission, UK)

- Official page: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarships/ (read 2026-11-01)
- Award value:
  - Fees: "full fees are covered by agreement between the CSC and the UK university"
  - Airfare: "Approved airfare from the Scholar's home country to the UK and return"
  - Stipend: "Stipend (living allowance) at the rate of £1,712 per month"
  - A study travel grant is listed; no total value given.
- Eligibility: "only candidates from the eligible countries listed below can apply." India is on the list. Applicant must be "Be permanently resident in an eligible Commonwealth country."
- Application dates, next cycle: NOT CONFIRMED. Last cycle: "Applications for Commonwealth Master's Scholarships for the 2027/28 academic year are now closed." Closing: "16:00 BST on Tuesday 20 October." The page gives no year; 2026 is inferred because 20 October 2026 is a Tuesday. Opening date for that cycle not stated.
- How to apply: "Applications to the CSC are made using the application system, CSC Central." Applicants must also apply to a nominator. "The CSC does not accept direct applications for these scholarships."

## 4. Charles Wallace India Trust (CWIT)

- Official page: https://charleswallaceindiatrust.com/scholarships (read 2026-11-01). The Trust is a UK charity; the India scholarships are run with the British Council.
- Award value and coverage: "accommodation and living costs in the UK" and "a contribution of £700 towards the international travel costs." Not covered: "travel costs within India," "UK visa costs," IELTS costs.
- Note: "Open-call grants are described as 'worth more than £50k on average.'" This is not stated for the long-term awards.
- Eligibility (official page):
  - "Be aged between 28 and 38"
  - "Be an Indian citizen living in India"
  - Open to "artists and heritage professionals"; "CWIT prefers applicants with significant working experience."
  - "postgraduate study (one year course) at a UK university"
- Application dates, next cycle: NOT CONFIRMED. Page says "Applications are now closed" with no dates. Interview results "usually announced before the end of May" (no year).
- How to apply: application form with a Statement of Purpose of up to 2,000 words; IELTS certificate; evidence of unconditional offer; portfolio link where relevant; shortlisted candidates interviewed online.
- Note: the Trust site is https://charleswallaceindiatrust.com (home page read: "Every year CWIT offers two long term awards"; "These scholarships are for artists (all disciplines)"). The Oxford Ruskin School MFA route has its own contact (graduate@rsa.ox.ac.uk), not CWIT.
- Secondary (not used for figures): Birmingham, Yocket and other listings.
