# Immigration Health Surcharge: how it is charged (read on gov.uk, 2026-11-03)

Source: https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application/how-much-pay (read by Claude 2026-11-03)

| Item | Answer | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| Student yearly rate | £776 per year | "£776 per year for students, their dependants, and those on a Youth Mobility Scheme visa" |
| Visa longer than a year, up to 18 months | the yearly cost plus half the yearly cost | "the yearly cost plus half the yearly cost, if it's for 18 months or less" |
| Example: a 16-month visa | £776 + £388 = £1,164 | worked from the rule above |
| Visa of more than 6 months but less than a year | the cost of a full year | "you'll have to pay the cost of a full year" |

Why this matters: the surcharge is charged on the length of the visa, not the length of the course. The Home Office caseworker guidance says entry clearance is granted "for the length of the course plus the appropriate additional period" (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/student-route-caseworker-guidance/student-and-child-student). The exact extra period was not found on a page read in this session, so no page states how long the visa will be. £776 is therefore the minimum for a one-year master's, not the usual total.

Visa length for a 12-month course: NOT CONFIRMED.
