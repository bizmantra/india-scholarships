## Notes and conflicts

- **Proof of funds conflict:** gov.uk pages disagree. The financial evidence page body and the Immigration Rules Appendix (updated 29 October 2026) say £1,529 London / £1,171 outside London. The update notice on the same financial evidence page (dated 1 November 2024) says £1,483 / £1,136 for applications on or after 2 January 2025. The Immigration Rules figures are the rule text; confirm before budgeting.
- **Dependants (for reference only):** Appendix Student gives £845 per month (London) and £680 per month (outside London) for dependants, up to 9 months.
- **Student IHS:** the £776 figure is confirmed from gov.uk. Its effective date is not confirmed. Search snippets mentioned an older £470 figure from an NHS guide and a 6 Feb 2024 effective date; these were not verified on a page I could read, so they are not used.
- **Graduate Route:** the gov.uk pages do not mention a separate master's rule. The 2-year and 18-month lengths apply to the application date. Older figures (£700 fee, £624 surcharge, £822 fee) found in search are from old documents and are not used.
- **IELTS:** the search snippet claiming IELTS 6.0 equals B2 came from a source I did not open, so it is not used. A UKVI-specific IELTS test requirement is also not confirmed on a page I read.
- **Gov.uk fee page:** the Student visa fee is quoted from both the Student visa overview page and the fee table (effective 8 April 2026).
- **Pages not readable:** British Council postgraduate page returned HTTP 403. The Legislation page for SI 2024/55 returned empty content.
