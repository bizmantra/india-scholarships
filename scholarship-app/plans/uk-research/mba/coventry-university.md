## Notes and conflicts

1. The MBA course page does not state an accreditation body. Round 5 did not find one either. Do not publish an accreditation claim for this MBA.
2. The international apply table lists no September 2027 deadline. The course page lists September 2027 as a start date. This is the main gap for planning.
3. The India deposit rule is only partly stated. Punjab, Uttarakhand and Haryana pay full first-year tuition. Other states are not named, so the GBP 8,000 rule is an inference for them.
4. The Round 5 note about a course-page caveat ("for 2026-27 entry, guidance for 2027-28") did not appear in this read. The fee table is read from the 2027-28 view.
5. Round 5 said the Group Scholarship "covers 2026/27 or 2027/28 entry". That matches the page read today.
