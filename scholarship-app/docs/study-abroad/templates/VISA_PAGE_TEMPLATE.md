# Visa Guide Template

`/study-abroad/visas/{slug}`, stored in `sa_guides` with `kind = 'visa'`. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md) and the [guide template](GUIDE_PAGE_TEMPLATE.md). Links: [LINKING_RULES.md](LINKING_RULES.md) (Visa guide).

**The reader:** a student with an admission letter (or close to one) who must get a student visa from India, often anxious about rejection and money proof.

**The job of the page:** the exact, current process from India: documents, money proof, fees, appointment, interview, timelines, with every rule linked to the embassy, VFS or government source.

**Today (audit, 11 Oct 2026):** 11 visa pages; 3 are empty apart from the intro and a table; several overlap (Germany interview questions vs interview prep; F-1 master guide vs F-1 checklist vs DS-160 manual).

## Rules specific to visas

- Visa rules change often: `checked_at` must be within 30 days for the page to be marked current; the facts agent (recovery plan, "Study Abroad Facts") re-checks fees and amounts.
- Never promise outcomes ("guaranteed approval") or describe ways around official processes. "Hacks" is not used.
- Interview pages give sample questions as examples of what officers ask, and explain what a good answer shows; no scripted "model answers" to memorise.
