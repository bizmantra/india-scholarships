# Country List Template (universities, scholarships, visas, loans)

`/study-abroad/study-in/{country}/universities`, `/scholarships`, `/visas`, `/loans`. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md). Links: [LINKING_RULES.md](LINKING_RULES.md) (Country lists).

**The reader:** someone browsing options in one country.

**The job of the page:** a complete, sortable list with the facts needed to shortlist, plus enough guidance to choose. The universities list owns searches like "masters in Germany universities" (about 3,600 a month); it must be more than a list of names.

**Today (audit, 11 Oct 2026):** the Germany universities list is 344 words with one heading. Guides such as "Germany Master University Directory Hub" and "Top 15 German Public Universities" compete with it.

## Sections

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Intro** | 2–3 sentences: what is listed, how many, how the data is checked. |
| 2 | **How to choose** (universities and scholarships lists only) | 150–400 words of real guidance: what matters for Indian students (tuition vs free, subject match, GRE policy, city costs, intakes for universities; eligibility, amount and deadlines for scholarships). Absorbs the competing directory guides, which then redirect here. |
| 3 | **The list** (auto) | Universities: name, city, tuition for Indian students, subjects offered (links to comparison pages), GRE policy. Scholarships: name, amount, level, deadline. Visas: name, what it covers. Loans: page name and what it compares. Filters and sorting where the list is longer than 10. |
| 4 | **FAQs** (universities and scholarships lists only) | 3–5, e.g. "Which German universities don't need GRE for Indian students?" answered from the list data. |
| 5 | **Sources** | How the list data is checked, with dates. |

## Search

- Universities: "masters in {country} universities", "best universities in {country} for Indian students" (only claim "best" with a stated method).
- Scholarships: "scholarships to study in {country} for Indian students".
- Title example (under 65): "Universities in Germany for Indian Students: Fees & GRE".
