# Study Abroad Home Template

`/study-abroad`. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md). Links: [LINKING_RULES.md](LINKING_RULES.md) (Study Abroad home).

**The reader:** an Indian student or parent who has decided (or is deciding) to study abroad, often arriving from the main site's scholarship pages or menu.

**The job of the page:** get them to the right country, course comparison or university in one or two clicks, and show that this site gives straight, sourced answers without agency fees.
