## <a id="eligibility"></a>🎯 Uttar Pradesh State Eligibility & Income Limit Rules

> **Direct Answer:** Eligibility for Uttar Pradesh state scholarships requires **UP domicile** and enrollment in a recognized school/college. The annual family income cap for SC/ST applicants is **₹2.50 Lakhs/year**, while for OBC, General, and Minority applicants, the ceiling is **₹2.00 Lakhs/year**.

* **Pre-Matric Schemes (Class 9-10):** Must have passed previous class and be enrolled in a UP board/recognized school.
* **Post-Matric Schemes (Class 11-12 & Degree):** Must submit valid UP income certificate and Aadhaar-linked bank account details.

---

## <a id="documents"></a>📄 Mandatory Documents Checklist for UP Schemes

> **Direct Answer:** All UP scholarship applications require **Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate (issued by Tehsildar), Caste Certificate, Domicile Certificate, Marksheet of Previous Examination, Bank Passbook, and Fee Receipt** uploaded on `scholarship.up.gov.in`.

1. **Aadhaar Card** (Linked with mobile number & bank account for NPCI seed).
2. **Income Certificate** (Valid for 2025-2026 cycle).
3. **Caste & Domicile Certificate** (Sub-division officer / Tehsildar verified).
4. **Current Course Fee Receipt & Bonafide Certificate**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (UP State Hub)

### Q1. What is the official portal for Uttar Pradesh state scholarships?
**Answer:** The official online portal is `scholarship.up.gov.in`, managed jointly by the Social Welfare Department, Backward Classes Welfare Department, and Minority Welfare Department of Uttar Pradesh.

### Q2. What is the income limit for UP Post-Matric Scholarship 2026?
**Answer:** For SC/ST category applicants, the annual family income limit is **up to ₹2.50 Lakhs/year**. For OBC, General, and Minority category applicants, the limit is **up to ₹2.00 Lakhs/year**.
