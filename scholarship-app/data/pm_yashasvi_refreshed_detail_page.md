## ℹ️ About the Program

Complete guide to the PM YASASVI Scholarship 2026 on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). This flagship central government scholarship scheme provides robust financial support to meritorious students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT). The scheme is designed to cover tuition fees, hostel expenses, and stationery allowances for students studying in empanelled Top Class Schools across India.

---

## <a id="eligibility"></a>🎯 PM Yashasvi Scholarship Eligibility Criteria & Income Limit

> **Quick Answer:** To qualify for the **PM Yashasvi Scholarship 2026**, candidates must belong to designated **OBC, EBC, or DNT** categories, maintain an annual family income of **less than ₹2.50 Lakhs**, and be enrolled in Class 9 or Class 11 at an empanelled Top Class School.

### Eligibility Breakdown:

* **Academic Benchmarks & Education Level:**
  * Must be studying in **Class 9 (Pre-Matric)** or **Class 11 (Post-Matric)** in the 2026 academic year.
  * Must be enrolled in a designated **Top Class School** empanelled under the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
  * Must have passed the previous class (Class 8 for Class 9 applicants, or Class 10 for Class 11 applicants) in the first attempt.
* **Financial & Income Bar:**
  * Total annual family income from all sources must **not exceed ₹2,50,000 (₹2.50 Lakhs/year)**.
  * An official Income Certificate issued by a competent state authority (Tehsildar/SDM) is mandatory.
* **Category & Domicile:**
  * Must belong to **OBC (Other Backward Classes)**, **EBC (Economically Backward Classes)**, or **DNT (De-notified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Tribes)**.
  * Must be a permanent resident of India.
  * No age limit restrictions apply.

## <a id="benefits"></a>💰 Benefits & Financial Support Structure

> **Quick Answer:** The PM Yashasvi Scheme provides up to **₹75,000 per year for Class 9–10 students** and up to **₹1,25,000 per year for Class 11–12 students**, covering tuition, hostel, and academic allowances via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

| Category / Class Level | Annual Scholarship Amount | Covered Expenses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class 9 & Class 10** | Up to **₹75,000 / year** | School tuition fees, hostel fees, books, and stationery allowances |
| **Class 11 & Class 12** | Up to **₹1,25,000 / year** | School tuition fees, hostel charges, uniform, and educational supplies |

### Disbursement Mode:
All financial assistance is transferred via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** directly into the student's **Aadhaar-seeded bank account** through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## <a id="documents"></a>📄 Documents Required for PM Yashasvi Online Application

> **Quick Answer:** Applicants must upload clear scanned PDF/JPEG copies (under 200 KB) of their **Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate (< ₹2.5L), Caste Certificate, School Bonafide Certificate, and Class 8/10 Marksheet** during NSP registration.

### Mandatory Documents Checklist:

1. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar Card (Must be actively linked/seeded to the student's bank account).
2. **Income Certificate:** Valid Family Income Certificate showing annual income $\le$ ₹2.50 Lakhs (issued by Tehsildar/SDM for FY 2025–2026).
3. **Category Certificate:** Official Caste Certificate certifying OBC, EBC, or DNT status.
4. **Academic Marksheet:** Class 8 Marksheet (for Class 9 entry) or Class 10 Marksheet (for Class 11 entry).
5. **School Admission Proof:** Bonafide Certificate / School Fee Receipt confirming admission in an empanelled Top Class School.
6. **Bank Account Details:** Copy of bank passbook showing student name, account number, and IFSC code.

## <a id="apply"></a>📝 How to Apply Online for PM Yashasvi Scholarship on NSP Portal

> **Quick Answer:** Students can apply online by completing One Time Registration (OTR) on **scholarships.gov.in**, completing eKYC verification, selecting the *PM Yashasvi Top Class School Scheme*, uploading documents, and submitting the form before July 31.

### Step-by-Step Registration Guide:

* **Step 1:** Visit the official National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at `https://scholarships.gov.in/`.
* **Step 2:** Click on **'Apply for Scholarship'** and select **'Register'** if you are a new user.
* **Step 3:** Complete the **One Time Registration (OTR)** using your mobile number, Aadhaar details, and OTP verification.
* **Step 4:** Log in to your account, complete Face Authentication or eKYC verification, and activate your profile.
* **Step 5:** Navigate to **'Apply for Scholarship'**, select *PM Yashasvi Scholarship* under the *Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment*, and fill in personal, academic, and family income details.
* **Step 6:** Upload required documents (Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Marksheets, Bonafide Proof).
* **Step 7:** Submit the application before July 31, 2026, and save your Application ID to track online verification status.

---

### 🔄 Renewal Application Guidelines

> Existing beneficiaries do **not** need to register as new applicants. Log in to the National Scholarship Portal using your previous Application ID, select **'Apply for Renewal'**, upload your previous year marksheet showing successful promotion, and submit the form for automatic school re-verification.

## <a id="selection"></a>🏆 Selection Process & Merit Criteria

> **Quick Answer:** Selection for the PM Yashasvi Scholarship is merit-based, determined by the student's school board marks from the previous academic year and enrollment in empanelled Top Class Schools. **There is no separate entrance exam for the 2026 cycle.**

* **Board Marks Priority:** Applications are ranked based on marks secured in Class 8 or Class 10 examinations.
* **Empanelled School Mandate:** Only students enrolled in Top Class Schools notified by the Ministry on the NSP portal are eligible for final disbursement.
* **DBT Merit List:** Verified applications are forwarded to the Central Ministry, and funds are released directly via Direct Benefit Transfer.

## <a id="faqs"></a>❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the minimum family income limit to apply for PM Yashasvi Scholarship 2026?
**Answer:** The annual family income must be less than ₹2.50 Lakhs per annum. Students belonging to OBC, EBC, and De-notified Tribes (DNT) categories are eligible if they meet this income criterion.

### Q2. Is there a separate entrance exam for the PM Yashasvi Scholarship 2026?
**Answer:** No, there is no separate entrance exam for the 2026 cycle. Selection is based solely on the student's school board marks from the previous year and their enrollment in an empanelled Top Class School listed on the NSP portal.

### Q3. Can I apply if I already receive another government scholarship?
**Answer:** No, a student cannot hold two government scholarships simultaneously. If you are already receiving another government scholarship, you must choose one and cannot avail of the PM Yashasvi Scholarship in addition to it.
