## Key Observations

1. **Yashasvi Traffic Collapse Post-Closure**:
   - Up until the deadline on **July 31st**, the PM Yashasvi scholarship page was the site's primary traffic driver, bringing in a peak of **1,158 sessions** on July 27th and constituting up to **32.9%** of the entire site's traffic on Sunday, July 26th.
   - Once applications officially closed on July 31st, Yashasvi traffic declined steeply:
     - **July 31st (Deadline Day)**: 766 sessions (16.3% share)
     - **August 1st (Day after)**: 492 sessions (11.2% share)
     - **August 2nd (Sunday)**: 244 sessions (12.8% share)
   - This represents a **77.8% drop** from its previous Sunday peak (1,103 sessions on July 26th down to 244 sessions on August 2nd).

2. **Direct Impact on Overall Site Traffic**:
   - The drop in Yashasvi traffic alone accounts for **60% of the entire Sunday-to-Sunday traffic decline** on the website:
     - Sunday, July 26th Total Sessions: **3,357**
     - Sunday, August 2nd Total Sessions: **1,909**
     - Overall Sunday-to-Sunday Drop: **1,448 sessions**
     - Yashasvi Sunday-to-Sunday Drop: **859 sessions** (1,103 down to 244)
     - **Yashasvi Contribution to Drop**: **59.3%**

3. **Other Scholarship Drops (Remaining 40%)**:
   - Non-Yashasvi sessions also dropped from **2,254** (July 26th) to **1,665** (August 2nd) — a drop of **589 sessions**.
   - This secondary drop is expected because many other state and central scholarship schemes (such as the initial rounds of several NSP and Corporate schemes) also set deadlines around July 31st. As these portal application windows closed, organic search query volume for "apply online", "last date", and "eligibility" fell sharply.
   - Traffic is expected to recover as soon as the next major wave of state portals (such as UP, MP, and Karnataka) open their fresh 2026-27 application cycles (usually beginning late August through September).
