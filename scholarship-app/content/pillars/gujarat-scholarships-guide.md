Gujarat's scholarship system runs mostly through one website, but that website hosts many separate schemes — and Gujarat also runs several named programs beyond the basic category forms that are easy to miss. This guide explains how it all fits together.

## One main website, many separate schemes

Almost all of Gujarat's scholarships are applied for on **digitalgujarat.gov.in**. Within that one site, the core schemes split two ways, the same pattern used by most other states:

- **By category**: separate Post-Matric forms for **SC**, **ST**, **OBC/SEBC**, and **Minority** students.
- **By class stage**: a combined **Pre-Matric** scheme (Class 1-10) for SC/ST/OBC students, and separate **Post-Matric** schemes (Class 11 onwards, including college) by category.

**What this means for you:** Combine your category with your class stage to find the right form — a Post-Matric form doesn't apply if you're still in Class 8, and a Pre-Matric form doesn't apply once you reach Class 11.

## The Post-Matric amounts genuinely differ by category

This is easy to miss. The Post-Matric schemes aren't just different application forms with the same benefit — the actual amounts differ:

- **SC Post-Matric**: 100% of compulsory tuition fees, plus maintenance allowance up to ₹1,200/month.
- **ST Post-Matric**: 100% of compulsory tuition fees, plus maintenance allowance up to ₹1,200/month.
- **OBC/SEBC Post-Matric**: tuition fee coverage, plus maintenance allowance up to ₹750/month.
- **Minority Post-Matric**: tuition fee coverage, plus maintenance allowance up to ₹500/month.

Always check your specific scheme's page for the exact current amount, rather than assuming it matches another category.

## Named schemes beyond the basic category forms

Gujarat runs several additional schemes worth checking, each with its own specific eligibility:

- **MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana)** — merit-based, for General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS students scoring 80%+ in Class 10 or 12. Pays up to ₹2,00,000/year for undergraduate study as a 50% tuition reimbursement, plus a book grant and hostel allowance.
- **Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students** — a separate SC-specific scheme from Class 11 through PhD, reimbursing 100% of compulsory tuition fees plus a maintenance allowance up to ₹1,200/month depending on course and hostel status.
- **Namo Saraswati Vigyan Sadhna Yojana** — for girl students who scored 50%+ in Class 10 and joined the Science stream in Class 11. Pays ₹25,000 total over Class 11 and 12.
- **Mukhyamantri Kanya Kelavani Nidhi (MKKN)** — for female students pursuing MBBS at government or aided colleges, up to ₹4 lakh/year.
- **Scholarship to the Disabled Students (Divyang)** — for students with disabilities, Class 1 through postgraduate, ₹1,500 to ₹4,500/year depending on class and hostel status. Requires a valid disability certificate (UDID).
- **Swami Vivekanand Stipend Scheme** — for students in ITI and technical training courses, ₹400/month (₹4,800/year).
- **Food Bill Assistance to SEBC/SC/ST Hostellers** — ₹1,500/month for 10 months (₹15,000/year) to cover hostel food costs for medical and engineering students.
- **Gujarat NMMS (National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship)** — ₹1,000/month for Class 9 through 12, for General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS students meeting the means-cum-merit criteria.

## Documents you'll almost always need

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Gujarat Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, for SC, ST, OBC/SEBC, or Minority applicants.
4. **Family Income Certificate.**
5. **Latest marksheet**, matching your current class or stage.
6. **Bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar**, since most schemes pay through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
7. **Disability certificate (UDID)**, only if applying for the Divyang scholarship.

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check your category and class stage first** — Pre-Matric or Post-Matric, and which caste category applies to you.
2. **If you scored 80%+ in Class 10 or 12**, check MYSY regardless of category.
3. **If you're a girl in Class 11 Science**, check Namo Saraswati specifically.
4. **If you're a girl pursuing MBBS**, check MKKN.
5. **If you have a disability**, check the Divyang scholarship — it runs separately from the main category-based schemes.
6. **If you're in ITI or technical training**, check the Swami Vivekanand Stipend Scheme.
7. **Apply on digitalgujarat.gov.in** for most schemes, and keep your application number for status checks later.
