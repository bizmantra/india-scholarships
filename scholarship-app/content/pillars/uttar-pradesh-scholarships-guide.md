Uttar Pradesh runs its scholarships through one main website, but that website hosts many different forms — and picking the wrong one is a common, avoidable mistake. This guide explains how UP organizes its scholarships, so you land on the right form the first time.

## One website, many forms

Almost all UP scholarships are on **scholarship.up.gov.in**. But within that one site, forms are split two ways: by your **category group**, and by your **class stage**.

**Category groups:**
- **General, SC, and ST** — combined into one form. This is different from most states, which usually give SC and ST their own separate forms.
- **[OBC](/scholarships-for/obc)** — a separate form.
- **[Minority](/scholarships-for/minority)** (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsi, Jain) — a separate form.

**Class stages:**
- **Pre-Matric** — Class 1 to Class 10.
- **Post-Matric Intermediate** — Class 11 and Class 12.
- **Post-Matric Other-than-Intermediate** — undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and professional courses.

**What this means for you:** Combine your category group with your class stage to find the right form. A General category student in Class 12 needs a different form from a General category student in college, even though both are "General" category.

## Studying outside UP? There's a specific form for that

If you hold UP domicile but are studying in a **different state**, UP has a specific **Outside State Post-Matric** scholarship option. Use this instead of the regular in-state form.

## Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana works differently

This is a benefit scheme for girl children, not a typical yearly scholarship. Money is released at different life stages — from birth through higher education — rather than through a single yearly application. It runs on its own separate portal, **mksy.up.gov.in**, not the main UP scholarship portal. If you are researching this scheme, treat it as a separate, long-term benefit.

## AKTU students have their own welfare fund

If you study at a college affiliated with **AKTU** (Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University), there is a separate AKTU Student Welfare Fund you may be able to apply for, in addition to the regular UP scholarships.

## Beyond the main portal: 6 more schemes worth checking

The Pre-Matric/Post-Matric system isn't the only source of UP scholarship money. Several other departments run their own schemes, each with its own eligibility and portal:

- **UP Divyangjan Post-Matric Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement** — for students with 40%+ disability (valid UDID card required), Class 11 through PhD, worth up to ₹50,000 plus a monthly stipend. Run by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, separate from the main Social Welfare Department schemes.
- **UP Sanskrit Education Scholarship** — for students studying Sanskrit from Class 6 up to Acharya/postgraduate level. The scheme recently removed its ₹50,000 family income cap, so more students now qualify.
- **UP Chief Minister (CM) Fellowship Programme** — a postgraduate/PhD research fellowship from the Planning Department, worth up to ₹4,80,000 a year (monthly stipend plus field travel allowance), for scholars with 60%+ in their postgraduate degree.
- **UP CST Junior Research Assistantship (JRA)** — a PhD-track research assistantship from the Council of Science & Technology, worth up to ₹3,36,000 a year.
- **Lucknow University Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship** — a Student Welfare Fund grant of up to ₹15,000 for Lucknow University undergraduate and postgraduate students under an income ceiling of ₹3 lakh.
- **UP Purva Sainik Kalyan Educational Scholarship** — for children of ex-servicemen who top their Class 10 or Class 12 boards, worth up to ₹40,000, run by the UP Purva Sainik Kalyan Nigam (UPPSKNL).

**What this means for you:** Don't stop at the main scholarship.up.gov.in forms. If you have a disability, study Sanskrit, are a research scholar, attend Lucknow University, or your parent served in the armed forces, one of these separate schemes may apply on top of your regular scholarship.

## Documents UP almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Uttar Pradesh Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, for SC, ST, OBC, or Minority applicants.
4. **Income Certificate** — usually under ₹2 lakh or ₹2.5 lakh depending on the exact scheme.
5. **Fee receipt and admission registration number.**
6. **Previous year's marksheet.**
7. **Bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar**, for payment through **PFMS** (Public Financial Management System).

## How to find the right form — step by step

1. **Figure out your category group first**: General/SC/ST (combined), OBC, or Minority.
2. **Figure out your class stage**: Pre-Matric, Post-Matric Intermediate, or Post-Matric Other-than-Intermediate.
3. **Check if you study outside UP** — use the Outside State form if so.
4. **If you are a girl student**, also look into Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana as a separate, long-term benefit.
5. **Check if a department-specific scheme applies to you** — disability, Sanskrit studies, research, Lucknow University, or ex-servicemen's children — since these run separately from the main portal and are easy to miss.
6. **Keep your Aadhaar-bank link current**, since PFMS payment failures are one of the most common reasons scholarship money doesn't arrive on time.
