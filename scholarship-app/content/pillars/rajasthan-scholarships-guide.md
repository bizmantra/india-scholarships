Rajasthan uses one login for almost everything, called SSO. But that doesn't mean every scholarship works the same way — different departments manage different schemes behind that one login. This guide explains how it fits together, and two things that trip up a lot of students: Jan Aadhaar, and the state's Scooty schemes.

## One login (SSO), but different departments behind it

Almost every Rajasthan scholarship application starts with **SSO** — Rajasthan's Single Sign-On portal, used across many state government services, not just scholarships. But once you're logged in, which scheme you see and apply for depends on the department running it:

- **SJE** (Social Justice and Empowerment Department) — runs caste-based Post-Matric scholarships for [SC](/scholarships-for/sc), [ST](/scholarships-for/st), and [OBC](/scholarships-for/obc) students, plus a Pre-Matric scheme for OBC/SBC/EBC students. Managed at sje.rajasthan.gov.in, but you still log in through SSO.
- **Other departments** — run broader schemes like the Chief Minister Higher Education Scholarship (open to General, OBC, SC, ST, and EWS students) and the girls' Scooty schemes described below.

## Jan Aadhaar is not the same as Aadhaar

This is one of the most common points of confusion. **Jan Aadhaar** is a Rajasthan state family ID card. It is separate from your **national Aadhaar card**, issued by the central government.

Most Rajasthan scholarship forms ask for both numbers — your national Aadhaar and your Jan Aadhaar. Having one does not mean you automatically have the other. If you don't have a Jan Aadhaar card yet, you can apply for one at your nearest e-Mitra center.

## Two Rajasthan schemes give a scooter, not cash

Most scholarships pay money. Two Rajasthan schemes work differently — they give a **scooter (Scooty)** as the reward, usually tied to academic performance:

- **Devnarayan Girls Student Incentive Scooty Distribution Scheme** — for OBC girl students.
- **Kali Bai Bhil Meritorious Girls Scooty Scheme** — open to meritorious girl students across General, OBC, SC, ST, and EWS categories.

If you are a girl student in Rajasthan and see one of these schemes, don't expect a cash transfer — the incentive itself is a vehicle, meant to support daily travel to school or college.

## Documents Rajasthan almost always asks for

1. **Jan Aadhaar Card** — Rajasthan's own family ID, separate from national Aadhaar.
2. **National Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
3. **Caste Certificate**, issued or verified through an **e-Mitra** center.
4. **Income Certificate** for the current financial year.
5. **Current year college fee receipt.**
6. **Previous year's marksheet.**
7. **Bank account linked to Aadhaar (NPCI seeding)**, for Direct Benefit Transfer.

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Register on SSO first** — nearly every Rajasthan scholarship needs this login.
2. **Check your category.** SC, ST, and OBC students should look at the SJE Post-Matric schemes.
3. **Check the Chief Minister Higher Education Scholarship** if you don't fit a specific caste category, or want to check a broader merit-based option.
4. **If you are a girl student**, also check the Devnarayan and Kali Bai Bhil Scooty schemes.
5. **Get both your Aadhaar and Jan Aadhaar numbers ready**, along with e-Mitra documents, before you start any form.
