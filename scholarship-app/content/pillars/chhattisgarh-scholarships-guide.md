Chhattisgarh does not use one rule for its scholarships. Some schemes depend on your caste, some on your family's poverty status, some on your marks, and one is specifically for girl children. This guide explains which is which.

## Four different websites, four different rules

- **postmatric-scholarship.cg.nic.in** — caste-based Post-Matric scholarships, with separate forms for [SC](/scholarships-for/sc), [ST](/scholarships-for/st), and [OBC](/scholarships-for/obc) students.
- **highereducation.cg.gov.in** — the **BPL Scholarship**, based on poverty status rather than caste (see below).
- **education.cg.nic.in** — hosts both the **Chief Minister Gyan Protsahan Yojana** (merit-based, open to all categories) and **Kanya Saksharta Protsahan Yojana** (a literacy incentive specifically for SC and ST girl students).
- **wcd.cg.gov.in** — run by the Women and Child Development department, including **Noni Suraksha Yojana** (see below).

**What this means for you:** Don't assume every Chhattisgarh scheme checks the same thing. Some check your caste certificate, some check your BPL card, and some check your marks.

## The BPL scholarship uses a poverty card, not a caste certificate

Most caste-based schemes ask for a caste certificate. The **BPL Scholarship** is different — it is based on your family holding a valid **BPL (Below Poverty Line) card**, and it is open to General, OBC, SC, ST, and EWS students as long as that condition is met. If you don't have a caste certificate but your family has a BPL card, this scheme is worth checking.

## Noni Suraksha Yojana is a welfare scheme, not a typical scholarship

"Noni" means girl child in the local Chhattisgarhi dialect. **Noni Suraksha Yojana** comes from the Women and Child Development department, not the education department, and it works differently from a normal yearly scholarship application. Treat it as a separate girl child welfare benefit, and check its specific rules rather than assuming it works like the Post-Matric or BPL schemes.

## Documents Chhattisgarh almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Chhattisgarh Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, only for SC, ST, or OBC Post-Matric schemes.
4. **BPL Card**, only if applying for the BPL scholarship.
5. **Income Certificate** for the current financial year.
6. **Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets.**
7. **Bank account linked to Aadhaar (NPCI seeding).**

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check your category first.** SC, ST, or OBC students should start with the Post-Matric scholarship on postmatric-scholarship.cg.nic.in.
2. **Check if your family has a BPL card**, even if you don't fit a caste category — the BPL scholarship may still apply.
3. **Check your marks.** The Chief Minister Gyan Protsahan Yojana is merit-based and open more broadly.
4. **If you are an SC or ST girl student**, also check Kanya Saksharta Protsahan Yojana.
5. **If you are researching girl child welfare benefits**, look into Noni Suraksha Yojana separately, since it works differently from a yearly scholarship.
