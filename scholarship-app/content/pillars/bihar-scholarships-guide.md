Bihar's scholarship system can look confusing at first, mainly because its two biggest systems — PMS and Medhasoft — each show up under two different web addresses. This guide clears that up, along with one important scheme that isn't actually a scholarship.

## PMS and Medhasoft each have two web addresses — both are real

This is the single biggest source of confusion for Bihar students:

- **PMS** (Post-Matric Scholarship), for SC, ST, BC, and EBC students, is reachable at both **pmsonline.bihar.gov.in** and **pmsonline.bih.nic.in**. There is also a PMS Mobile App.
- **Medhasoft**, which hosts schemes like Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana, Chief Minister Medhavi Yojana, and the graduation stage of Kanya Utthan Yojana, is reachable at both **medhasoft.bihar.gov.in** and **medhasoft.bih.gov.in**.

Both addresses for each system are genuine. If a link you find looks slightly different from one you saw before, it doesn't necessarily mean it's fake — check the exact link shared for your specific scheme.

## The Student Credit Card is a loan, not a scholarship

This is an important distinction. The **Bihar Student Credit Card**, under the 7 Nishchay Yuva Upmission program, gives students access to funds for education — but it is an **education loan**, not a scholarship. You are expected to repay it over time. Don't confuse it with PMS or Medhasoft schemes, which don't need to be repaid.

## Bihar's income limit is a little higher than usual

Many states cap family income at around ₹2.5 lakh per year for scholarship eligibility. Bihar commonly uses a limit closer to **₹3 lakh**. Always check the exact figure for your specific scheme, since it can still vary between schemes.

## Documents Bihar almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Bihar Domicile (Residential) Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, issued by a Revenue Officer or Circle Officer (CO).
4. **Income Certificate**, usually under ₹3 lakh.
5. **Bonafide Certificate from your institute**, including your fee structure.
6. **Class 10 or previous exam marksheet.**
7. **Bank account linked with Aadhaar (NPCI seeding).**

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check your category first.** SC, ST, BC, and EBC students should start with PMS.
2. **Check Medhasoft** for Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana, Chief Minister Medhavi Yojana, or Kanya Utthan Yojana at the graduation stage.
3. **Decide if you actually want a loan or a scholarship** before applying to the Student Credit Card scheme — remember, it must be repaid.
4. **If you're a sportsperson**, check the separate Bihar State Sports Authority scholarship.
5. **Get your Revenue Officer / CO-issued caste certificate ready early**, since local document processing can take time.
