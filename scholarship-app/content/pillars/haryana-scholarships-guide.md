Haryana's scholarship system combines caste-based Post-Matric schemes with several named merit, sports, and stipend programs. This guide walks through all of them.

## Post-Matric scholarships are split by category

Like most states, Haryana runs **separate** Post-Matric forms by category:

- **Haryana Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students**
- **Haryana Post-Matric Scholarship for BC/OBC Students**

Alongside this, the **Consolidated Stipend Scheme for SC Students** provides an additional stipend specifically for SC students — check both if you're SC, since they're structured differently and a genuinely eligible student may qualify for more than one.

## Named schemes beyond the category-based ones

- **Haryana State Merit Scholarship** — based on academic performance, not caste category.
- **Kalpana Chawla Chhatravritti Yojna** — for girl students, named after the astronaut; check its specific class/stage eligibility.
- **Dr. Ambedkar Medhavi Chhattar Sansodhit Yojana** — a named merit scheme; check its specific eligibility criteria before applying.
- **Haryana State Sports Scholarship Scheme** — a dedicated program for student-athletes, separate from academic or category-based schemes.

## Documents you'll almost always need

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Haryana Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, for SC or BC/OBC applicants.
4. **Family Income Certificate.**
5. **Latest marksheet**, matching your current class or stage.
6. **Bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar**, for payment.

## How to find the right scheme

1. **Check your category first** — SC and BC/OBC students each have a dedicated Post-Matric form, and SC students should also check the Consolidated Stipend Scheme.
2. **If you're a strong academic performer**, check the Haryana State Merit Scholarship regardless of category.
3. **If you're a girl student**, check the Kalpana Chawla Chhatravritti Yojna specifically.
4. **If you're a student-athlete**, check the Haryana State Sports Scholarship Scheme.
5. **Check central schemes too** — our [guide to how state scholarships work](/pillars/state-scholarships-how-they-work-guide) explains how state and central (NSP) systems fit together.
