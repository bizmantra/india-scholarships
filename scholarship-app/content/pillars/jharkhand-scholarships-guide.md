Jharkhand's scholarship system is smaller than some states, but it's organized clearly once you know where to look. This guide covers the main portal and the state's specific schemes.

## e-Kalyan is the main portal

Most Jharkhand state scholarships — particularly Post-Matric support for **SC**, **ST**, and **OBC** students — are applied for through **e-Kalyan**, the state Welfare Department's scholarship portal. Start here if you're a category-eligible student in Jharkhand.

## Beyond the main portal: two more schemes worth checking

- **Marang Gomke Jaipal Singh Munda Overseas Scholarship** — for **ST students** pursuing higher education abroad, separate from e-Kalyan's in-India schemes.
- **Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana** — a merit-based scheme that isn't limited to a specific caste category, worth checking even if you don't qualify for a category-specific scheme.

## Documents you'll almost always need

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Jharkhand Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, for SC, ST, or OBC applicants.
4. **Family Income Certificate.**
5. **Latest marksheet**, matching your current class or stage.
6. **Bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar**, for payment.

## How to find the right scheme

1. **Check e-Kalyan first** if you're SC, ST, or OBC — it's the primary portal for most Jharkhand state scholarships.
2. **If you're ST and planning to study abroad**, check the Marang Gomke Jaipal Singh Munda Overseas Scholarship specifically.
3. **If you don't fit a caste category**, check Mukhyamantri Shiksha Protsahan Yojana for merit-based eligibility.
4. **Also check central schemes** — our [guide to how state scholarships work](/pillars/state-scholarships-how-they-work-guide) explains how state and central (NSP) systems fit together.
