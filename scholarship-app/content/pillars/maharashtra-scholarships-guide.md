Maharashtra's scholarship system is bigger and more fragmented than it first looks — MahaDBT alone runs across two websites, and several other schemes sit entirely outside it. This guide sorts out where to look and what to expect.

## MahaDBT is actually two websites

**MahaDBT** (Maharashtra Direct Benefit Transfer) is the main system most Maharashtra state scholarships run through. But it is split across **two separate sites**:

- **mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in**
- **mahadbt2.maharashtra.gov.in**

Different schemes are hosted on different versions. If you can't find your specific scholarship on one site, check the other before assuming something is broken.

## Maharashtra has more category groups than most states

Beyond the usual General, [SC](/scholarships-for/sc), [ST](/scholarships-for/st), and [OBC](/scholarships-for/obc), Maharashtra also uses:

- **VJNT** — Vimukta Jati and Nomadic Tribes (also called Denotified and Nomadic Tribes).
- **SBC** — Special Backward Class.
- **EBC** — Economically Backward Class.

**What this means for you:** Check your caste certificate carefully — it states your exact category, and Maharashtra schemes are often written for one of these specific groups rather than a general "SC/ST/OBC" bucket.

## You may need two caste-related documents, not one

This trips up a lot of students. Maharashtra usually asks for both:

1. A **Caste Certificate**, confirming your category.
2. A **Caste Validity Certificate**, a separate document issued by a Caste Scrutiny Committee, confirming that your caste certificate is genuine.

Having only the caste certificate is often not enough — check the specific scheme's document list before assuming you're ready to apply.

## Schemes that sit outside MahaDBT entirely

Not everything runs through MahaDBT:

- **TRTI** (Tribal Research and Training Institute) runs its own separate fellowship portal, fellowship.trti-maha.in, specifically for ST students.
- **NMMS** (National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship) is managed through mscenmms.in, run by the state exam council — it's merit-based, decided through an entrance exam.
- Some universities run their own schemes, like the **SPPU Earn and Learn Scheme** at Savitribai Phule Pune University, only open to that university's own students.

## Documents Maharashtra almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Maharashtra Domicile Certificate.**
3. **Income Certificate**, issued by your Tahsildar.
4. **Caste Certificate**, for SC, ST, OBC, VJNT, or SBC applicants.
5. **Caste Validity Certificate** — remember, this is separate from your caste certificate.
6. **Previous year's marksheet and fee receipt.**
7. **Bank passbook linked with Aadhaar (NPCI seeding).**

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check your exact category** on your caste certificate — SC, ST, OBC, VJNT, SBC, EBC, EWS, or General.
2. **Start with MahaDBT**, and check both versions of the site if you can't find your scheme on the first one.
3. **If you are ST and interested in research**, also check TRTI's separate fellowship portal.
4. **If you did well in a merit exam**, check NMMS separately from MahaDBT.
5. **Prepare both your Caste Certificate and Caste Validity Certificate early** — the second one can take time to arrange.
