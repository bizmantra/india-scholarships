Madhya Pradesh does not run one scholarship system — it runs at least three, each on a different website. This guide explains what each one is for, so you don't waste time on the wrong one.

## Three different systems, three different websites

- **MPTAAS** — caste-based scholarships for SC, ST, and OBC students, each with a separate Post-Matric form. Despite the name (Tribal Affairs Automation System), it covers more than just tribal students. Portal: tribal.mp.gov.in/MPTAAS. See our [MPTAAS portal guide](/guides/mptaas-mmvy-mp) for step-by-step help.
- **MMVY** (Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana) — a merit-based scholarship open to General, [OBC](/scholarships-for/obc), [SC](/scholarships-for/sc), [ST](/scholarships-for/st), and EWS students with good exam results. Portal: medhavikalyan.mp.gov.in.
- **State Scholarship Portal** (scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in) — a group of separate welfare schemes, including Gaon Ki Beti Yojana (for rural girl students), Pratibha Kiran Yojana, Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Shiksha Protsahan Yojana, and the MP Vikramaditya Free Education Scheme.

**What this means for you:** If you are SC, ST, or OBC, start with MPTAAS. If you scored well in your last exam regardless of category, also check MMVY. If neither fits, look through the general schemes on the state scholarship portal.

## MPTAAS isn't only for tribal students

The name MPTAAS — Tribal Affairs Automation System — makes it sound like it is only for ST (tribal) students. It is not. MPTAAS also runs the Post-Matric Scholarship for SC students and the Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC students, each as its own separate form. The name reflects which department runs the system, not who can use it.

## Ladli Lakshmi Yojana works differently

Most schemes on this list are yearly applications tied to your current class or course. **Ladli Lakshmi Yojana is different.** It is a long-term government savings scheme for girl children — money is invested over time by the government and released at key life stages, including for education. Think of it as a separate, long-term benefit rather than something you apply for every year like a normal scholarship.

## Documents Madhya Pradesh almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Samagra ID** — an 8 or 9 digit number used across MP government services. You will need this for most scholarship forms, including MPTAAS.
3. **Caste Certificate**, issued by the MP government in digital format, only if applying for MPTAAS.
4. **Income Certificate** for the current financial year.
5. **Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets.**
6. **Current college admission receipt** and course fee details.
7. **Bank passbook**, linked to Aadhaar (NPCI seeding), for Direct Benefit Transfer.

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check your category first.** SC, ST, or OBC students should start with MPTAAS.
2. **Check your marks**, regardless of category — MMVY is merit-based and open to more students.
3. **If you are a girl student from a rural area**, check Gaon Ki Beti Yojana on the state scholarship portal.
4. **If you don't fit any of the above**, browse the other schemes on scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in — several are open to General category students.
5. **Get your Samagra ID ready** before you start any application — most MP forms ask for it alongside Aadhaar.
