Punjab's scholarship system splits mainly by caste category for its largest schemes, plus a few smaller merit-based and targeted programs. This guide covers all of them.

## Post-Matric scholarships are split by category

Punjab runs **separate** Post-Matric scholarship forms depending on your category:

- **Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students**
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC/BC Students**

These are two different applications — make sure you're applying to the one that matches your category, not assuming one form covers everyone.

## Merit-based and targeted schemes worth checking

- **Punjab Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme** — a broader merit-based scheme, not limited to one caste category.
- **Dr. Hargobind Khurana Scholarship Scheme** — a merit/subject-focused scholarship, separate from the caste-based Post-Matric schemes.
- **Attendance Scholarship for SC Girls** — rewards regular school attendance for SC girl students, rather than exam marks.

## Documents you'll almost always need

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Punjab Domicile / Resident Certificate.**
3. **Caste Certificate**, for SC or OBC/BC applicants.
4. **Family Income Certificate.**
5. **Latest marksheet**, matching your current class or stage.
6. **Bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar**, for payment.

## How to find the right scheme

1. **Check your category first** — SC and OBC/BC students each have their own dedicated Post-Matric form.
2. **If you don't fit those categories, or want to check further options**, look at the Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme and Dr. Hargobind Khurana Scholarship Scheme for merit-based eligibility.
3. **If you're an SC girl student**, also check the Attendance Scholarship specifically.
4. **Check central schemes too** — our [guide to how state scholarships work](/pillars/state-scholarships-how-they-work-guide) explains how state and central (NSP) systems fit together.
