Kerala doesn't put its scholarships in one place — different departments run different schemes, and which portal you need depends on the type of scheme, not just your category. This guide sorts out which is which.

## Different departments, different portals

- **DCE** (Directorate of Collegiate Education) — merit-based schemes open to General category students too, including the District Merit Scholarship and Suvarna Jubilee Merit Scholarship. Portal: dcescholarship.kerala.gov.in.
- **e-Grantz** — specifically for [SC](/scholarships-for/sc), [ST](/scholarships-for/st), OEC, and [OBC](/scholarships-for/obc) students. Portal: egrantz.kerala.gov.in.
- **NSP** (National Scholarship Portal) — some Central Government schemes, like certain minority pre-matric and disability scholarships, are applied for here even though you live in Kerala. Portal: scholarships.gov.in.
- **Minority Welfare Department** — Kerala's own state-run minority scheme, separate from NSP. Portal: minoritywelfare.kerala.gov.in.
- **KSCSTE** (Kerala State Council for Science, Technology and Environment) — a science-focused merit scholarship. Portal: kscste.kerala.gov.in.

**What this means for you:** Don't assume every Kerala scholarship is on one website. Match the type of scheme — merit, caste-based, central, minority, or science-specific — to the right portal.

## What does OEC mean?

You will see **OEC** (Other Eligible Communities) listed alongside SC, ST, and OBC in Kerala scheme eligibility. This is a category specific to Kerala, for certain communities that qualify for similar welfare benefits as SC/ST students. If you're not sure whether you fall under OEC, check with your local Village Officer or look at your caste certificate.

## Not everything is on a Kerala website

This surprises some students: certain schemes for Kerala students — like some minority pre-matric scholarships and disability (PH) scholarships — are actually Central Government schemes, applied for through the **National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in)**, not a Kerala-specific site. Don't assume a scheme must be missing just because you can't find it on a Kerala government website — check NSP too.

## Documents Kerala almost always asks for

1. **Aadhaar Card**, linked to your mobile number and bank account.
2. **Kerala Domicile Certificate.**
3. **Income Certificate**, issued by your **Village Officer**.
4. **Caste Certificate**, for SC, ST, OEC, or OBC applicants.
5. **SSLC (Class 10) and Higher Secondary (Class 12) marksheets.**
6. **College admission receipt and student ID card.**
7. **Bank passbook with Aadhaar (NPCI seeding).**

## How to find the right scheme — step by step

1. **Check the type of scheme first**: merit-based (DCE), caste-based (e-Grantz), central (NSP), minority (state or central), or science-specific (KSCSTE).
2. **If you are SC, ST, OEC, or OBC**, start with e-Grantz.
3. **If you don't fit a caste category**, check the merit-based schemes on DCE.
4. **If you are a minority student**, check both the state Minority Welfare Department scheme and NSP — they're separate.
5. **Prepare your Village Officer income certificate early**, since this document is needed across almost every Kerala scheme.
