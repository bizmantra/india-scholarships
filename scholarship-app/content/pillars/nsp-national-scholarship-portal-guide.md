If you've searched for "NSP scholarship," you've probably run into a mix of results — some about registration, some about status checks, some about specific schemes. That's because NSP isn't one scholarship. This guide explains what it actually is, which schemes use it, and why applications commonly get stuck.

## What NSP actually is

The **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** — scholarships.gov.in — is a shared online platform run by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), in coordination with the scholarship-granting ministries and departments. It hosts application forms for **over a hundred separate scholarships**, mostly funded by the central government, plus UGC and AICTE schemes for higher education.

You don't apply "for NSP." You register once on NSP, and then apply to one or more of the specific schemes it hosts — for example, the **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students**, the **Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities**, or the **National Overseas Scholarship for SC/ST Students**.

## Central vs State: which portal should you actually use?

This is the single biggest source of confusion in NSP-related searches.

- **Central government scholarships** (funded by ministries in Delhi, or by UGC/AICTE) almost always route through NSP.
- **State government scholarships** (funded by your own state) usually route through your state's own portal — Odisha's scholarship.odisha.gov.in, Karnataka's SSP portal, and so on — **not** NSP.
- A handful of state Post-Matric schemes for SC/ST/OBC students do route through NSP for disbursal, since the central government co-funds them. Always check your specific scheme's page to confirm which portal applies to you — don't assume based on the scheme's name alone.

If you're SC or ST, our [SC/ST Scholarships in India guide](/pillars/sc-st-scholarships-india-guide) breaks down exactly which of your schemes are central (NSP) and which are state-run.

## How OTR (One-Time Registration) works

NSP uses a system called **OTR — One-Time Registration**. Instead of filling a fresh application from scratch every year, you register once with Aadhaar-based **Face-RD biometric verification**, which gives you a permanent NSP student ID.

- **First-time applicants** complete the full OTR process — Aadhaar verification, personal details, and document upload.
- **Renewal-year applicants** log in with their existing OTR ID and complete a shorter renewal form, without redoing biometric verification.

For the exact click-by-click steps, see our [NSP OTR Registration & Login Guide](/guides/nsp).

## What schemes are actually hosted on NSP?

NSP covers a wide range of central schemes across categories, including:

- **Merit and means-based**: Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students, Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF), UGC Junior Research Fellowship (JRF).
- **Caste-based**: Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students, National Fellowship & Scholarship for Higher Education of ST Students, PM-YASASVI Top Class Education Scheme for OBC/EBC/DNT.
- **Minority-focused**: Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities, Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for Professional and Technical Courses.
- **Special categories**: AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students, Ishan Uday Special Scholarship for the North Eastern Region.

This is not the full list — new schemes are added and closed each cycle. Check the full [NSP scheme overview](/scholarships/national-scholarship-portal-nsp) for the current set.

## Why NSP applications get rejected or stuck

Two issues account for most of the confusion:

1. **Bank account not Aadhaar-seeded.** NSP pays scholarship money through the NPCI mapper system, which requires your bank account to be linked to your Aadhaar as the active DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) account. If it isn't, your application can be fully approved and still show no payment.
2. **Stuck at Institute verification.** After you submit, your Institute Nodal Officer (INO) — a designated staff member at your school or college — has to verify your application before it moves forward. If your status shows "Pending at Institute" for a long time, the delay is on your institution, not a portal error. Contact your INO directly.

## Step-by-step: how to actually use NSP

1. **Check if your scheme is central or state-run first** — don't register on NSP if your specific scholarship is actually a state scheme with its own portal.
2. **Complete Aadhaar-seeding on your bank account before you apply**, not after — this is the step most students skip and regret later.
3. **Complete OTR** (first-time) or **log in with your existing OTR ID** (renewal), following the [NSP guide](/guides/nsp).
4. **Apply to every scheme you're eligible for** — NSP allows you to apply to more than one scheme in the same cycle, as long as you meet each one's individual eligibility.
5. **Follow up with your Institute Nodal Officer** if your status doesn't move for several weeks — this is the most common real-world bottleneck.
