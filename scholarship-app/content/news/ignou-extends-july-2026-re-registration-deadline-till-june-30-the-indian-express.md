Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) has extended its re-registration deadline. Students for the July 2026 academic session now have more time. The final date for re-registration is July 31, 2026.

This extension applies to all eligible programs. It covers courses offered via Open and Distance Learning (ODL). Online mode programs are also included.

**Who Can Re-register?**

This process is for currently enrolled students. It allows them to enroll for their next semester or year. This is not for new admissions to IGNOU.

Eligibility requires you to be an existing student. You must have completed at least one prior academic session.

**How to Re-register**

Visit the official IGNOU Samarth portal. The link is ignou.samarth.edu.in or onlinerr.ignou.ac.in. Log in using your enrollment number and password.

Carefully select your courses for the upcoming session. Verify all your personal and academic details.

**Fees and Payment**

Program fees vary by course. Pay the required fee online. You can use debit/credit card, Net Banking, or UPI.

After successful payment, download your confirmation page. Keep a copy for your records.

**Important Reminders**

Ensure your chosen payment method is active. Digital payments are processed in real-time. Do not close your browser during payment.

If your bank account is mapped for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), ensure it's active. While not directly for scholarship, general financial transactions benefit from this.

Missing the deadline could mean losing an academic session. A late fee of INR 200 might be charged.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for IGNOU July 2026 Re-registration Deadline Extended to July 31 successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.
