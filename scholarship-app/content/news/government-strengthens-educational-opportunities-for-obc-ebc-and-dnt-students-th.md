The Government of India is strengthening educational opportunities. It is doing this through the PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India (PM-YASASVI). This scheme supports students from OBC, EBC, and DNT communities.

**Eligibility Details**

Students from OBC, EBC, or DNT categories can apply. Their family's annual income must be below ₹2.5 lakhs. The scholarship is for studies from Class 9 up to post-graduation. Students must be enrolled in "Top Class" schools or institutions. Selection is now based on merit from previous class marks.

**Financial Support**

The scholarship provides substantial financial aid. Students in Classes 9 and 10 can receive up to ₹75,000 annually. For Classes 11 and 12, this amount increases to ₹1,25,000 per year. This covers tuition, hostel fees, and other essential charges.

**Application Process**

Applications are submitted through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The online application window opened on June 1, 2026. The last date for students to apply is August 31, 2026.

**Important Dates (2026-27)**

*   Online Application Deadline: August 31, 2026
*   Defective Application Verification: By November 15, 2026
*   Institute Verification: By November 15, 2026
*   DNO/SNO/MNO Verification: By November 30, 2026

**Documents Needed**

Prepare your Aadhaar Card and passport-size photograph. Also, keep educational marksheets and income certificate ready. A domicile certificate and caste certificate are also required. Disability certificates, if applicable, and bank details are necessary. Admission proof and fee details might also be needed.

**Aadhaar and DBT Reminder**

Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. This is crucial for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of funds. Incorrect linking can delay scholarship payments. Update your bank account details on NSP accurately.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for PM-YASASVI Scheme Boosts Education for OBC, EBC, DNT Students successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the deadline to apply for PM-YASASVI Scheme Boosts Education for OBC, EBC, DNT Students?
Applications generally open between August and October for the 2026–27 academic session. Students are advised to submit forms before the official closing date to allow sufficient time for institute verification.

### 2. Can students with income above ₹2.5 Lakh per annum apply?
Income limits vary by category. SC/ST/OBC post-matric schemes usually mandate an annual family income ceiling of ₹2.50 Lakh, whereas merit-cum-means and corporate schemes may allow higher income thresholds.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Log in to the official portal dashboard using your Application ID and DOB. Navigate to **Track Application Status** or **PFMS Payment Status** to check real-time approval stages.
