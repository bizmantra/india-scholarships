The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has officially opened the National Scholarship Portal for the 2026–27 academic year.

Students nationwide can now submit fresh and renewal applications across central ministries, UGC, and AICTE schemes.

The unified platform coordinates educational assistance from primary schooling through post-doctoral research programs.

Explore our comprehensive [National Scholarship Portal guide](/guides/nsp) for navigation instructions and login assistance.

---

## Mandatory One-Time Registration (OTR) Protocol

The central administration mandates the completion of One-Time Registration before applying for individual schemes.

The OTR system assigns an enduring 14-digit reference number to every verified applicant.

This permanent credential eliminates repeated document uploads across successive academic renewal cycles.

Applicants must download the official Aadhaar FaceRD mobile application on an Android smartphone.

Biometric facial recognition verifies the student identity against the central UIDAI population database.

The registration requires an active mobile number linked with Aadhaar to receive authentication one-time passwords.

Candidates can consult our [Post-Matric Scholarships portal guide](/scholarships/national-scholarship-portal-nsp) for specific scheme terms.

## Major Central Schemes Open for Application

Multiple government departments disburse scholarship grants through the unified NSP digital infrastructure.

1. **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS)**:
   Supports college and university undergraduates in the top 20th percentile of their 12th board exams.
   Review criteria on the [Central Sector Scholarship Page](/scholarships/central-sector-scholarship-csss).

2. **Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students**:
   Covers full tuition fees and living expenses at premier institutions like IITs, IIMs, and NITs.

3. **AICTE Technical Scholarships**:
   Offers financial assistance to girl students and differently-abled scholars pursuing technical degree programs.

Students exploring funding across all territories can browse the [all India scholarship hub](/scholarships-in/all-india) for comprehensive lists.

## Timeline and Verification Milestones for 2026–27

The National Scholarship Portal adheres to strict operational deadlines across distinct verification tiers.

| Operational Phase | Official Stakeholder | Anticipated Calendar Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student Registration** | Fresh & Renewal Applicants | Open through November 30, 2026 |
| **Institute Verification (L1)** | College Nodal Officers (INO) | Active through December 15, 2026 |
| **District Verification (L2)** | District Nodal Officers (DNO) | Active through December 31, 2026 |
| **PFMS DBT Disbursement** | Public Financial Management System | Scheduled from January 2027 onward |

Applications remaining unverified at the college level after institutional deadlines lapse automatically.

## Key Requirements for Successful Submission

Students must prepare mandatory credentials before starting the online application form:

* Valid 12-digit Aadhaar number with updated biometric records
* Active mobile phone registered in the student name
* Scanned copy of previous academic examination marksheet
* Current financial year family income certificate issued by a competent revenue officer
* Valid domicile certificate certifying state residency
* Active savings bank account seeded with the NPCI mapper for Direct Benefit Transfer

Ensure your bank account possesses active NPCI mapper seeding to prevent transfer failures on PFMS.
