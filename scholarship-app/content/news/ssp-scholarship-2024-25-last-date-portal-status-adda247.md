## Karnataka SSP Scholarship 2026-27: Apply Now

The Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP) is now accepting applications. Students can apply for various scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year. This portal simplifies the application process for many state schemes.

The SSP Scholarship helps students from diverse backgrounds. This includes SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and economically weaker sections. Financial aid ranges from INR 2,500 to INR 13,500. The amount depends on your category and course.

### Key Dates for 2026-27 Applications

Application deadlines vary by department. For Social Welfare Department post-matric scholarships, the last date is January 31, 2027. Backward Classes and Tribal Welfare Departments' post-matric schemes close on August 31, 2026. Minority Welfare Department scholarships have a deadline of September 30, 2026. Pre-matric scholarships generally close on September 30, 2026.

### Eligibility Requirements

You must be a permanent resident of Karnataka. Most schemes require a minimum of 50% marks in the previous exam.

Income limits vary by category and scheme. For SC/ST students, the income limit is typically up to ₹2.5 lakh. Some post-matric SC students may qualify with income up to ₹10 lakh for partial benefits. For OBC/Minorities, the income limit is usually ₹1 lakh or ₹2 lakh per annum.

### How to Apply

Visit the official SSP portal: ssp.karnataka.gov.in. New users must create an account using their Aadhaar number, email, and mobile number.

Complete Aadhaar e-KYC as it is mandatory. Upload necessary documents like caste, income certificates, and marksheets. Post-matric students also need e-attestation of documents by their college.

### Aadhaar and DBT Reminders

Scholarship funds are disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Your bank account must be linked with Aadhaar (NPCI mapped). Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded correctly to avoid payment delays. You can check your NPCI mapping status.

### Checking Application Status

After applying, track your scholarship status online. Visit ssp.karnataka.gov.in and use your Student SATS ID. This allows you to monitor your application progress.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for Karnataka SSP Scholarship 2026-27: Application Details and Deadlines successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the deadline to apply for Karnataka SSP Scholarship 2026-27: Application Details and Deadlines?
Applications generally open between August and October for the 2026–27 academic session. Students are advised to submit forms before the official closing date to allow sufficient time for institute verification.

### 2. Can students with income above ₹2.5 Lakh per annum apply?
Income limits vary by category. SC/ST/OBC post-matric schemes usually mandate an annual family income ceiling of ₹2.50 Lakh, whereas merit-cum-means and corporate schemes may allow higher income thresholds.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Log in to the official portal dashboard using your Application ID and DOB. Navigate to **Track Application Status** or **PFMS Payment Status** to check real-time approval stages.
