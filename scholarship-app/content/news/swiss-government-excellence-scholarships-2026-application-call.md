The Federal Commission for Scholarships for Foreign Students has opened the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships for the 2026–27 cycle.

Indian researchers, postgraduates, and postdoctoral scholars can apply for fully funded doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships in Switzerland.

The scholarship program fosters international scientific exchange and research collaboration between Switzerland and over 180 countries.

Detailed financial and eligibility breakdowns are available on our [Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships page](/scholarships/swiss-government-excellence-scholarships).

---

## Program Types and Eligible Academic Streams

The Swiss Confederation provides three distinct funding tracks for eligible Indian academic researchers:

1. **Ph.D. Scholarships**:
   Designed for highly qualified postgraduate scholars pursuing full-time doctoral research at a Swiss cantonal university or federal institute.
   Tenure is initially approved for 12 months with extensions up to 36 months based on academic progress.

2. **Postdoctoral Scholarships**:
   Aimed at researchers holding a recent Ph.D. degree undertaking advanced research projects for a 12-month period.

3. **Research Fellowships**:
   Open to postgraduates who intend to carry out short-term specialized laboratory research in Switzerland without formal degree enrollment.

Scholars exploring broader European scientific mobility can also examine the [DAAD WISE research internship](/scholarships/daad-wise-research-internship-germany) in Germany.

## Comprehensive Financial Benefits and Monthly Stipend

The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship represents one of the most generous research awards globally.

| Benefit Category | Entitlement Amount | Operational Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Living Stipend** | CHF 2,450 per month (~₹2,38,000/month) | Covers personal living expenses in Switzerland |
| **Health Insurance** | 100% Comprehensive Coverage | Mandatory Swiss statutory health insurance covered by FCS |
| **Travel Allowance** | Lump-Sum Return Airfare | Fixed air travel reimbursement paid upon arrival in Switzerland |
| **Housing Allowance** | CHF 600 One-Time Grant | Supports initial apartment rental security deposit requirements |
| **Public Transit Card** | Swiss Half-Fare Travelcard | Provides 50% discount on Swiss federal railway journeys |

The scholarship does not impose tuition charges at federal institutions like ETH Zurich or EPFL.

## Mandatory Requirement: Host Professor Acceptance Letter

The single most critical requirement for Indian applicants is securing an academic host professor in Switzerland.

The Federal Commission for Scholarships rejects any application lacking a signed, formal letter of acceptance.

Applicants must contact full professors at Swiss universities whose research specializations align with their proposed projects.

The invitation letter must confirm that the professor agrees to supervise the research and provide laboratory infrastructure.

Begin outreach early, as Swiss faculty members receive numerous prospective research inquiries from global applicants.

## Step-by-Step Application Timeline

* **Application Window**: Official application dossiers must be requested via the Swiss Embassy in New Delhi.
* **Submission Deadline**: The Embassy of Switzerland in India accepts completed application dossiers through mid-November 2026.
* **Selection Announcement**: The Federal Commission for Scholarships publishes final selection decisions by May 2027.
* **Fellowship Commencement**: Selected fellows begin their research residency in Switzerland starting September 1, 2027.
