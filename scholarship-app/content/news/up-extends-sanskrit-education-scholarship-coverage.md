The Government of Uttar Pradesh has announced a major expansion of its Sanskrit Education Scholarship scheme to support students studying in traditional Sanskrit schools, Gurukuls, and degree institutions across the state.

**Key Policy Changes & Income Ceiling Removal**

Under the revised guidelines, the UP State Government has completely abolished the previous family income cap of ₹50,000 per annum. This reform ensures that all enrolled Sanskrit students meeting the attendance threshold can receive financial support without income restrictions.

**Scholarship Amounts & Student Coverage**

The scheme covers students across multiple educational stages:

* **Prathama (Classes 6–7)**: ₹50 per month
* **Prathama (Class 8)**: ₹75 per month
* **Purva Madhyama (Classes 9–10)**: ₹100 per month
* **Uttar Madhyama (Classes 11–12)**: ₹200 per month
* **Shastri & Acharya (UG & PG)**: ₹200 to ₹250 per month

For full eligibility rules and document details, check the [UP Sanskrit Education Scholarship guide](/scholarships/up-sanskrit-shiksha-scholarship).

**Eligibility & Attendance Norms**

To receive the monthly stipend, students must be enrolled in recognized Sanskrit institutions in Uttar Pradesh, maintain at least 75% attendance, and secure minimum 50% passing marks in their previous annual examinations. Payments are processed through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) linked to Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.

**Application Information**

Applications are submitted online via the official Uttar Pradesh state portal (`scholarship.up.gov.in`) during the state scholarship window. Students must submit hard copies of their online application to their school principal and District Inspector of Schools (DIoS) for institutional verification.
