**Karnataka SSP Scholarship 2026-27: Apply Now!**

The Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP) has opened applications for the 2026-27 academic year. This includes both Pre-Matric and Post-Matric scholarships. Students residing in Karnataka can apply online for various schemes.

**Important Dates to Note**

Pre-Matric scholarships across most departments close on September 30, 2026. For Post-Matric schemes, deadlines vary by department. Backward Classes Welfare and Tribal Welfare close on August 31, 2026. Minorities Welfare Department closes on September 30, 2026. The Social Welfare Department's deadline is January 31, 2027.

**Who Can Apply? (Eligibility Highlights)**

Applicants must be Karnataka residents. Scholarships are available for SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and EWS categories. Students from Class 1 up to PhD, including professional courses, are eligible. Family income limits apply, typically up to ₹2.5 lakh for many schemes. However, some schemes for particular categories or courses have higher limits.

**Application Process Explained**

The application process is entirely online through the SSP portal. First, complete a One-Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) if you are an OBC, SC, or ST student. Then, create your SSP account using your Aadhaar number. E-KYC is a mandatory step for identity verification. Accurately fill in academic and certificate details. Finally, upload e-attested documents, review, and submit your application.

**Key Documents Required**

Prepare your Aadhaar card, linked to your bank account for DBT. You will also need your SATS ID (for Pre-Matric). An income certificate and caste certificate with valid RD numbers are essential. Keep your bank passbook and previous year's marks card ready. Bonafide or admission certificates are also required.

**Aadhaar and DBT Reminders**

Aadhaar seeding with your bank account is crucial. This ensures direct benefit transfer (DBT) of scholarship funds. Only one bank account can be actively linked for DBT. Verify your Aadhaar-bank link status using the mAadhaar app or your bank. If you have multiple accounts, the most recently linked one for DBT will be used. Make sure your biometrics are unlocked on Aadhaar for e-KYC. SSP automatically verifies caste, income, and religion details.

**Official Portals**

The main SSP portal is ssp.karnataka.gov.in. For Post-Matric scholarships, use ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for Karnataka SSP Scholarships 2026-27: Registration Open for Pre & Post-Matric Schemes successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the deadline to apply for Karnataka SSP Scholarships 2026-27: Registration Open for Pre & Post-Matric Schemes?
Applications generally open between August and October for the 2026–27 academic session. Students are advised to submit forms before the official closing date to allow sufficient time for institute verification.

### 2. Can students with income above ₹2.5 Lakh per annum apply?
Income limits vary by category. SC/ST/OBC post-matric schemes usually mandate an annual family income ceiling of ₹2.50 Lakh, whereas merit-cum-means and corporate schemes may allow higher income thresholds.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Log in to the official portal dashboard using your Application ID and DOB. Navigate to **Track Application Status** or **PFMS Payment Status** to check real-time approval stages.
