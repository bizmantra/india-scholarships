The Government of Karnataka has issued a mandatory notification for all students applying on the State Scholarship Portal (SSP).

Kutumba Family ID validation is now required for both fresh and renewal post-matric applications.

The Kutumba database automatically pulls verified family income, caste, and residency details across Karnataka departments.

**Key Requirements for SSP Karnataka Applicants**

Students must ensure their family details are correctly registered in the Kutumba portal:
- **Kutumba ID Verification**: Enter your 8-digit or 10-digit Kutumba Family ID during SSP login.
- **E-Attestation Portal**: Upload Class 10/12 marksheets, caste certificate RD numbers, and income certificate RD numbers.
- **SATS Student ID**: School and PUC students must link their 9-digit SATS ID.

View complete Karnataka scheme requirements on our [SSP Karnataka Scholarship Page](/scholarships/ssp-pre-matric-post-matric-scholarship-karnataka).

**Step-by-Step SSP Application Process**

1. Visit the official SSP Karnataka portal at ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in.
2. Log in using your Student Account ID and password.
3. Fetch your validated family data by providing your Kutumba ID.
4. Verify your E-Attestation numbers for academic marksheets and fee receipts.
5. Submit the application and download the physical verification acknowledgment slip.

**Action Required for Mismatched Kutumba Data**

If your Kutumba record shows incorrect family income or missing family members, visit your nearest Bangalore One or Karnataka One center.

Update your Kutumba family card details before the official portal closing date.
