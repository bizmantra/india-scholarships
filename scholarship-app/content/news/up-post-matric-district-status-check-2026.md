The Social Welfare Department of Uttar Pradesh has updated the application status portal for 2026 applicants.

Post-Matric (Dashmottar) students across General, SC, ST, OBC, and EWS categories can now view official DWO status.

Verification status reports confirm whether institute verification, attendance percentage, and caste documents are cleared.

**How to Check UP Scholarship Status Online**

Students can check their status using their registration credentials:
1. Visit the official UP scholarship portal at scholarship.up.gov.in.
2. Click on the **Student** tab and select **Fresh Login** or **Renewal Login**.
3. Choose your category (Post-Matric Intermediate or Post-Matric Other Than Inter).
4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Password.
5. Click **Check Current Status** to view the District Scholarship Committee approval report.

View full eligibility guidelines on our [UP Post-Matric Scholarship Page](/scholarships/up-post-matric-scholarship-dashmottar).

**Understanding District Welfare Officer (DWO) Status Messages**

- **Verified / Recommended by District Scholarship Committee**:
  Your application is fully approved. Scholarship amount is queued for direct benefit transfer via PFMS.

- **Rejected by District Scholarship Committee**:
  Check the specific rejection reason displayed on screen (such as income ceiling mismatch, missing attendance, or non-matching marks).

- **Pending at District Scholarship Committee**:
  Your application is currently under physical document verification by district authorities.

**Remedial Actions for Rejections**

Students whose applications show document discrepancies must submit correction representation letters at their college.

Ensure your bank account is active and linked with Aadhaar NPCI seeding to avoid transfer failures.
