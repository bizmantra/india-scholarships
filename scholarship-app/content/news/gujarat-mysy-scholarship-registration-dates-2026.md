The Education Department of Gujarat has officially launched fresh and renewal registrations for MYSY 2026-27.

Eligible students can submit online applications through the official Digital Gujarat portal.

This flagship scheme provides financial support to diploma, degree, engineering, and medical students across Gujarat.

**Key MYSY Eligibility and Percentile Rules**

Under the MYSY scheme, students must meet specific academic cutoff criteria:
- **Degree/Engineering**: Minimum 80 percentile or above in Class 12 Board examination.
- **Diploma to Degree (D2D)**: Minimum 65% or above in Diploma engineering.
- **Income Ceiling**: Annual family income must not exceed ₹6,00,000 per annum.

Learn full scheme eligibility and document lists on our [MYSY Scholarship Page](/scholarships/mukhyamantri-yuva-swavalamban-yojana-mysy).

**Financial Benefits Offered**

Selected scholars receive significant financial assistance:
- **Medical (MBBS/BDS)**: 50% of tuition fee up to a maximum of ₹10,00,000 over course duration.
- **Engineering / Professional**: 50% of tuition fee up to a maximum of ₹50,000 per year.
- **Hostel Support**: ₹1,200 per month for students residing in non-government hostels.
- **Book and Equipment Allowance**: One-time grant of ₹5,000 for engineering and ₹10,000 for medical streams.

**How to Register on Digital Gujarat Portal**

1. Visit the official Digital Gujarat web portal.
2. Create a new user profile using your mobile number and Aadhaar card.
3. Select Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY) from the scheme directory.
4. Upload Class 10/12 marksheets, income certificate, and college admission fee receipt.
5. Get documents verified at designated Help Centers before the final deadline.
