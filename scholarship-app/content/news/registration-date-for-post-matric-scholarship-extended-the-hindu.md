**Important Update: Post-Matric Scholarship Applications for 2026-27**

Post-matric scholarship registrations are now open nationwide. Students should check new deadlines for the 2026-27 academic year. This follows previous extensions for the 2025-26 cycle in some states.

**National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Deadlines**

The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) opened applications on June 1, 2026. The final date for most post-matric scholarship applications is October 31, 2026. This covers schemes from various central ministries. Fresh applications began mid-July 2026. Institute verification closes around November 15, 2026. Payments are expected from December 2026 to February 2027.

**Key Eligibility**

These scholarships are for SC, ST, OBC, minority, and disabled students. Annual family income limits are generally ₹2.5 Lakh. There are plans to increase this to ₹4.5 Lakh from 2026-27.

**State-Specific Scholarship Updates**

**Telangana:** The state government will directly credit scholarships to student accounts. The ePass portal (telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) will accept applications year-round. This applies to all eligible categories.

**Bihar:** Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) applications opened July 15, 2026. The last date to apply is September 15, 2026. Portals are pmsonline.bihar.gov.in (BC/EBC) and scstpmsonline.bihar.gov.in (SC/ST).

**Karnataka:** Registrations for 2026-27 are currently active. Deadlines vary by department and scheme. Many close by August 31 or September 30, 2026. The Social Welfare Department deadline is January 31, 2027. Apply via ssp.karnataka.gov.in.

**Odisha:** Post-matric applications for 2026-27 are expected in late November.

**Uttar Pradesh:** Pre-Matric registrations for 2026-27 are open now. Post-Matric Inter applications should open by August 2026. Check scholarship.up.gov.in for updates.

**Important Reminders for Applicants**

Always register on the official National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). Also, check your respective state scholarship portals for local schemes. Ensure your Aadhaar number is correctly linked to your bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Scholarship funds will be credited directly. Timely application helps avoid last-minute issues.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for Post-Matric Scholarship Deadlines Extended for 2026-27 Applications successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the deadline to apply for Post-Matric Scholarship Deadlines Extended for 2026-27 Applications?
Applications generally open between August and October for the 2026–27 academic session. Students are advised to submit forms before the official closing date to allow sufficient time for institute verification.

### 2. Can students with income above ₹2.5 Lakh per annum apply?
Income limits vary by category. SC/ST/OBC post-matric schemes usually mandate an annual family income ceiling of ₹2.50 Lakh, whereas merit-cum-means and corporate schemes may allow higher income thresholds.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Log in to the official portal dashboard using your Application ID and DOB. Navigate to **Track Application Status** or **PFMS Payment Status** to check real-time approval stages.
