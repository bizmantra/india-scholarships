The Government of Maharashtra has opened the Aaple Sarkar DBT (MahaDBT) portal for 2026–27 scholarship applications.

Students enrolled in post-matric, degree, diploma, medical, engineering, and polytechnic courses can now apply online.

Multiple state welfare departments disburse tuition fee waivers, exam fee freeships, and monthly maintenance allowances through this single window.

**Key Schemes Administered on the MahaDBT Portal**

1. **Government of India Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students**:
   Provides 100% tuition and examination fee reimbursement plus maintenance allowance for scheduled caste scholars.
   Review terms on our [MahaDBT Post-Matric Scholarship Page](/scholarships/mahadbt-post-matric-scholarship-maharashtra).

2. **Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC)**:
   Offers 50% tuition and exam fee support for General and Economically Weaker Section students pursuing higher education.
   Check guidelines on the [Maharashtra EBC Post-Matric Page](/scholarships/maharashtra-ebc-postmatric).

3. **Post-Matric Tuition Fee and Examination Fee Freeship (OBC/VJNT/SBC)**:
   Offers fee waivers based on annual family income ceilings verified by tahsildar income certificates.

## Detailed Step-by-Step MahaDBT Application Procedure

To register and submit your scholarship application on the MahaDBT portal without verification delays, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **User Registration & Profile Creation**:
   - Visit the official MahaDBT portal at mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
   - Click on **New Applicant Registration**.
   - Create your username and password.
   - Complete OTP verification with your registered mobile number and email.

2. **Aadhaar Authentication & Profile Completion**:
   - Log in using your credentials and select **Aadhaar Authentication**.
   - Complete OTP-based Aadhaar validation.
   - Enter personal details, permanent Maharashtra address, and caste certificate barcode numbers.
   - Upload your valid income certificate issued by a competent Sub-Divisional Officer or Tahsildar.

3. **Academic Details & Course Selection**:
   - Enter current college enrollment details, course name, admission year, and CAP allotment round.
   - Provide past qualification marks and board roll numbers.

4. **Scheme Selection & Document Upload**:
   - Navigate to the **All Schemes** tab.
   - Choose the appropriate department (Social Justice, Tribal Development, or Higher Education).
   - Select your eligible scholarship scheme and upload college fee receipts and domicile certificates.

5. **Final Form Submission & College Forwarding**:
   - Review all uploaded records on the preview page.
   - Click **Submit Application**.
   - Download the application summary PDF.
   - Submit the application copy with supporting documents to your college scholarship clerk.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Active Aadhaar-Linked Bank Account**: Students must have a single bank account mapped with the NPCI mapper. Scholarship funds will be transferred directly via Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) to the seeded bank account.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the application deadline for MahaDBT 2026-27?
Applications for the 2026–27 academic year remain open from August through October 31, 2026. Colleges will complete institutional verification in subsequent cycles.

### 2. What is the family income limit for MahaDBT post-matric scholarships?
For SC and ST students, the annual family income ceiling is ₹2.50 Lakh. For OBC, EBC, and VJNT schemes, the income limit is up to ₹8.00 Lakh for tuition freeship schemes.

### 3. Can students from outside Maharashtra apply on MahaDBT?
No. Applicants must be domicile residents of Maharashtra and possess a valid Domicile Certificate issued by state authorities.
