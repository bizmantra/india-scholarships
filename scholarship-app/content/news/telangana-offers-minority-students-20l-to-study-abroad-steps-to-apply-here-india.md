The Telangana Minorities Welfare Department provides a significant scholarship. It is called the Chief Minister's Overseas Scholarship Scheme. This scheme offers up to ₹20 Lakhs. It also includes a one-way airfare of ₹60,000. This support helps minority students study abroad.

**Eligibility Details**

Applicants must be Telangana residents. They should belong to a minority community. This includes Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, and Parsis. The age limit is 35 years as of July 1st. Family income must be below ₹5 Lakhs annually. Only one child per family can apply.

Students need at least 60% marks. This applies to their qualifying degree. A valid TOEFL/IELTS and GRE/GMAT score is mandatory. Admission to an accredited foreign university is also required. Finally, applicants must possess a valid passport.

**Application Process and Deadlines**

Applications are submitted online. Visit the Telangana ePASS scholarship portal. Look for "Overseas Scholarship Services". Select the Chief Minister's Overseas Scholarship Scheme. Complete the online registration form. Upload all necessary documents.

The application period for Spring Season 2026 has closed. The final deadline was July 15, 2026. Admissions between January 1, 2026, and June 30, 2026, were eligible. Students should prepare for future application cycles.

After online submission, original documents must be submitted. Take them to District Minorities Welfare Offices. Parents' physical presence is required for verification. Using fictitious certificates will lead to criminal action.

**Important Reminders for Online Applications:**

Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account. This mapping should be with NPCI. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) requires this. Errors can cause payment delays. Double-check all entered information.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply for Telangana Overseas Scholarship: ₹20 Lakh for Minority Students successfully without errors or rejections, follow this structured procedural checklist:

1. **Official Portal Access & Registration**:
   - Navigate to the designated official state/national scholarship portal.
   - Click on **New Student Registration / One-Time Registration (OTR)**.
   - Enter your active mobile number, Aadhaar number, and email address to complete OTP verification.

2. **Form Filling & Category Selection**:
   - Log in using your generated Application ID and password.
   - Fill in personal details, caste category, annual family income, and current academic course enrollment.
   - Select your target scheme name accurately from the scheme drop-down menu.

3. **Document Uploading**:
   - Upload scanned copies of required documents in PDF or JPEG format (file size typically under 200 KB).
   - Ensure the document details match your Aadhaar card and academic certificates exactly.

4. **Institution Verification & Final Submission**:
   - Preview the completed application form to verify all fields.
   - Click **Final Submit** and download the acknowledgment receipt.
   - Submit a hard copy of the printed form along with original document photocopies to your school/college scholarship officer for institute-level verification.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aadhaar Bank Seeding Mandatory**: Ensure your bank account is seeded with Aadhaar via NPCI mapping. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship funds will be credited only to Aadhaar-seeded accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the deadline to apply for Telangana Overseas Scholarship: ₹20 Lakh for Minority Students?
Applications generally open between August and October for the 2026–27 academic session. Students are advised to submit forms before the official closing date to allow sufficient time for institute verification.

### 2. Can students with income above ₹2.5 Lakh per annum apply?
Income limits vary by category. SC/ST/OBC post-matric schemes usually mandate an annual family income ceiling of ₹2.50 Lakh, whereas merit-cum-means and corporate schemes may allow higher income thresholds.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Log in to the official portal dashboard using your Application ID and DOB. Navigate to **Track Application Status** or **PFMS Payment Status** to check real-time approval stages.
