Wipro Consumer Care and Lighting, in partnership with Wipro Cares, has opened the 2026–27 intake for the Santoor Women's Scholarship Program.

The initiative supports meritorious young women from underprivileged backgrounds who aspire to pursue higher education.

Selected candidates receive an annual financial assistance grant of ₹30,000 per year throughout their undergraduate studies.

Read complete scheme details, terms, and eligibility rules on our [Santoor Women's Scholarship page](/scholarships/santoor-womens-scholarship).

---

## Detailed Eligibility Requirements

The Santoor Scholarship maintains specific educational and regional eligibility parameters to assist deserving female scholars:

1. **Target Group**: Open exclusively to female students who passed Class 12 in the current academic session.
2. **Schooling Background**: Applicants must have passed Class 10 from a local government school.
3. **Higher Secondary**: Must have passed Class 12 from a government school or government junior college.
4. **Current Enrollment**: Must be enrolled in the first year of a full-time undergraduate degree program.
5. **Geographical Scope**: Open to students residing and studying in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, or Chhattisgarh.

Candidates pursuing technical degrees like B.Tech or B.Arch can also explore the [Legrand Empowering Tomorrow Scholarship](/scholarships/legrand-empowering-tomorrow-scholarship) for tuition assistance.

Review additional regional educational initiatives on the [Andhra Pradesh scholarships hub](/scholarships-in/andhra-pradesh).

## Financial Grant Structure and Use of Funds

The Santoor Scholarship grant provides meaningful financial relief to scholars and their families.

| Financial Parameter | Program Provision |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Annual Scholarship Grant** | ₹30,000 per annum |
| **Tenure of Support** | Up to 3 years (duration of undergraduate degree) |
| **Total Financial Value** | Up to ₹90,000 across course completion |
| **Permitted Expenses** | Tuition fees, textbooks, hostel charges, and study materials |
| **Disbursement Mode** | Direct electronic transfer to verified student bank account |

Scholars can utilize the grant to purchase laptops, technical stationery, and specialized academic materials.

Consult the [National Scholarship Portal guide](/guides/nsp) for comparative central scholarship norms.

## Required Documentation for Online Application

Applicants must prepare digitized copies of supporting documents before beginning their submission:

* Recent passport-size photograph of the student
* Class 10 passing certificate and marksheet from a local government school
* Class 12 marksheet from a government school or government junior college
* Undergraduate college admission fee receipt or institutional bonafide certificate
* Valid government identity proof (Aadhaar Card or Voter Identity Card)
* Student bank account passbook copy showing legible account number and IFSC

Failure to submit authentic government school credentials leads to application disqualification during screening.

## Application Timeline and Submission Procedure

1. **Portal Registration**: Visit the official scholarship portal at santoorscholarships.com or Buddy4Study and register with your email.
2. **Complete Bio and Academic Details**: Enter your schooling background, qualifying percentages, and undergraduate college enrollment data.
3. **Upload Scanned Certificates**: Attach clear copies of marksheets, bonafide slips, and your active bank passbook.
4. **Review and Submit**: Submit the form online before the official closing date in late November 2026.
5. **Telephonic Screening**: Shortlisted applicants undergo telephonic verification and background validation prior to fund disbursal.
