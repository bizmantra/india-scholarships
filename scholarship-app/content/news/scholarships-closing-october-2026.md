October is among the busiest months in the Indian scholarship calendar.

Dozens of central government schemes, state fee-reimbursement portals, private CSR foundations, and foreign master's grants close their annual intake windows between October 1 and October 31, 2026.

Missing an October deadline usually means waiting an entire year for the next cycle.

We have compiled verified scholarships closing this month across government portals, corporate trusts, state initiatives, and international fellowships.

## What to Do Right Now

Before reviewing the deadlines below, complete these four procedural steps:

1. **Prioritize by Closing Date**: Do not assume October 31 applies to every scheme. Flag early deadlines first, such as the Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship on October 5.
2. **Obtain Institutional Verification Early**: College schemes require verification by your Institute Nodal Officer (INO). Colleges often close internal submissions 3–5 days ahead of official portal deadlines.
3. **Verify Aadhaar-NPCI Bank Seeding**: Government schemes disburse funds exclusively through Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB). Check your seeding status on the NPCI portal immediately.
4. **Collect Tehsildar Income Certificates**: Revenue departments take 7–14 days to issue digitally signed certificates. Income affidavits on stamp paper are rejected by most portals.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Portals like NSP experience severe traffic slowdowns in the final 48 hours before October 31. Complete your form and save your acknowledgment slip at least five days before the deadline.
