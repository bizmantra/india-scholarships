Planning higher education in Germany requires mandatory academic document authentication.

The Academic Evaluation Centre (Akademische Prüfstelle or APS) verifies all Indian educational credentials.

German universities require this verification certificate before issuing final enrollment letters.

German consulates also mandate this verification document when processing student visa applications.

Explore public university requirements in the comprehensive [Study in Germany Guide](/study-abroad/study-in/germany).

---

## What Is the Germany APS Certificate?

The APS is an official body operated by the German Embassy New Delhi.

It operates in close partnership with the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD).

Key features of the certificate:
- **Mandatory Requirement**: Indian applicants for bachelor's and master's degrees must secure verification.
- **Verification Purpose**: It validates that Indian academic degrees meet German higher education standards.
- **Digital Document**: Successful candidates receive a digitally signed PDF with a scannable QR verification code.
- **Validity Duration**: The certificate carries indefinite validity for the verified academic qualification.

Students can convert Indian marks into German grades using the [Bavarian Grade Calculator](/study-abroad/tools/bavarian-grade-calculator).

## Applicable Processing Fees and Payment Details

The Academic Evaluation Centre charges a uniform administrative document processing fee.

Payment rules and guidelines:
- **Standard Fee Amount**: **₹18,000** for standard undergraduate and master's credential evaluation.
- **Payment Method**: Online bank transfer using NEFT or RTGS to the official account.
- **Non-Refundable Policy**: The administrative fee is non-refundable once document evaluation begins.
- **Payment Record**: Retain your bank transaction reference number for online profile verification.

## Mandatory Document Checklist

Prepare legible color copies of all required academic records before submitting applications:

| Document Category | Document Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Identity Proof** | Valid Indian passport copy showing personal identification and address pages |
| **Secondary School** | Class 10 board marksheet and school passing certificate |
| **Higher Secondary** | Class 12 board marksheet and senior secondary certificate |
| **Bachelor's Degree** | All individual semester marksheets and consolidated academic transcripts |
| **Degree Certificate** | Official degree certificate or provisional passing certificate from university |
| **Institutional Contact** | Official email address of college dean, registrar, or department head |

## Step-by-Step Online Application Process

Follow this structured sequence to secure your verification certificate without avoidable delay:

1. **Online Account Creation**: Register a candidate profile on the official website (`aps-india.de`).
2. **Select Candidate Category**: Pick the category matching your completed academic degree.
3. **Pay Processing Fee**: Transfer **₹18,000** via net banking and record the transaction number.
4. **Submit Academic Transcripts**: Provide verified semester marksheets and institutional contact information.
5. **Courier Document Dossier**: Dispatch printed registration forms and certified copies to New Delhi.
6. **Professor Email Verification**: Notify university faculty to respond to official verification emails promptly.
7. **Download Verification PDF**: Download the secure digital certificate directly from your candidate portal.

## Processing Timelines and Seasonal Delays

Processing durations vary depending on document completeness and institutional responsiveness:
- **Standard Turnaround**: Verification normally requires between **3 to 6 weeks**.
- **Peak Admission Seasons**: Applications during peak months from May to August take up to **eight weeks**.
- **Early Preparation**: Initiate the verification process at least three months before university application deadlines.

Following document verification, applicants must open a German blocked account for visas.
