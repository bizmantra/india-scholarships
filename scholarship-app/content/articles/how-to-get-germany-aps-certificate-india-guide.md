Planning higher education in Germany requires mandatory academic document authentication.

The Academic Evaluation Centre (Akademische Prüfstelle or APS) verifies all Indian educational credentials.

Without this official verification, German universities cannot finalize enrollment. German consulates will also reject visa applications lacking this certificate.

## What Is the Germany APS Certificate?

The APS is an official body operated by the German Embassy New Delhi.

It operates in close partnership with the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD).

Key features of the certificate:
- **Mandatory Requirement**: All Indian applicants for bachelor's, master's, or doctoral studies must obtain verification.
- **Verification Purpose**: It confirms the authenticity of Indian marksheets and school leaving certificates.
- **Digital Format**: Successful applicants receive a secure, digitally signed PDF containing a scannable QR verification code.
- **Validity Period**: The certificate holds permanent validity for the verified academic degree.

## Applicable Processing Fees and Payment Details

The Academic Evaluation Centre charges a standard administrative verification fee.

Fee structure and payment guidelines:
- **Standard Fee Amount**: **₹18,000** for undergraduate and postgraduate credential verification.
- **Payment Method**: Online bank transfer via NEFT or RTGS to the official APS bank account.
- **Non-Refundable Policy**: The fee is non-refundable once document processing commences.
- **Payment Proof**: You must keep the official bank transaction reference number for online submission.

## Mandatory Document Checklist

Prepare clean color scans of all required academic credentials before registration:

| Document Type | Verification Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Identity Proof** | Valid Indian passport copy with minimum one year validity |
| **Secondary School** | Class 10 board marksheet and certificate |
| **Higher Secondary** | Class 12 board marksheet and passing certificate |
| **Bachelor's Degree** | All individual semester marksheets and consolidated transcript |
| **Degree Certificate** | Original degree certificate or official provisional passing certificate |
| **Professor Email** | Institutional email domain of college dean, registrar, or department head |

## Step-by-Step Online Application Process

Follow this structured workflow to secure your digital certificate without procedural delays:

1. **Online Registration**: Create a secure candidate account on the official portal (`aps-india.de`).
2. **Select Application Category**: Choose the appropriate category matching your highest completed qualification.
3. **Pay Processing Fee**: Transfer ₹18,00,0 via online banking and input your transaction identification number.
4. **Authorize DigiLocker Access**: Link your DigiLocker account to allow direct verification of digital marksheets.
5. **Courier Document Dossier**: Dispatch printed application forms and certified photocopies to the New Delhi office.
6. **Professor Email Verification**: Alert your university professors to respond promptly to verification inquiries.
7. **Digital Certificate Issuance**: Download your digitally signed PDF verification document directly from your candidate portal.

## Processing Timelines and Seasonal Delays

The verification timeline varies based on application volume and university response speed:
- **Standard Processing Window**: Verification normally requires **3 to 6 weeks**.
- **Peak Season Delays**: Applications submitted during peak admission months (May to August) can take up to **8 to 10 weeks**.
- **DigiLocker Advantage**: Applicants with verified DigiLocker records often receive approvals up to two weeks faster.

After obtaining verification, students must set up a German blocked account for visa issuance.
