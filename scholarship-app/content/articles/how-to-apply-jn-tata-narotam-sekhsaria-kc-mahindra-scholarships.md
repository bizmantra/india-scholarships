Indian students heading abroad face significant financial costs. Tuition fees and living expenses often exceed ₹40,00,000 for a two-year degree.

Commercial bank loans carry high interest rates and processing fees. Securing full foreign university scholarships can also be challenging.

Prestigious Indian trusts offer a practical funding alternative. They provide low-interest loan scholarships, partial gift grants, and direct funding awards.

Review all verified options on the [IndiaScholarships Study Abroad Hub](/study-abroad).

## 1. JN Tata Endowment Loan Scholarship

The JN Tata Endowment is one of India's oldest educational trusts. Founded in 1892, it supports Indian graduates pursuing postgraduate education abroad.

Selected scholars receive loan assistance up to **₹10,00,000**. The trust can also award up to **₹7,50,000** for top academic performers.

Key guidelines:
- **Eligible Courses**: Master's, PhD, and postdoctoral programs in all academic disciplines.
- **Academic Requirement**: Minimum 60% marks in a recognized Indian undergraduate degree.
- **Interest Terms**: Repayment starts between the third and seventh year after disbursement. Simple interest of 2% applies only if repayments miss the scheduled timeline.
- **Application Window**: Applications open annually between January and March on the official portal.

Scholars are evaluated through an online diagnostic test and a formal technical interview. Read full eligibility details on the [JN Tata Endowment Scholarship Page](/scholarships/jn-tata-endowment-scholarship).

## 2. Narotam Sekhsaria Postgraduate Scholarship

The Narotam Sekhsaria Foundation awards interest-free loan scholarships for premier postgraduate degrees.

The foundation provides up to **₹20,00,000** to Indian students admitted to top-ranked global universities.

Key highlights:
- **Repayment Terms**: 100% interest-free loan assistance.
- **Moratorium Period**: Repayment begins one year after course completion.
- **Repayment Schedule**: Students repay the principal amount over four annual installments.
- **Eligible Fields**: Pure Sciences, Applied Sciences, Social Sciences, Law, Management, and Architecture.
- **Age Limit**: Candidates must be Indian citizens under 30 years of age.

Selection involves two rigorous assessment stages. First, the foundation reviews academic transcripts, SOPs, and reference letters. Shortlisted candidates attend personal interviews with subject-matter experts in Mumbai.

View full application steps on the [Narotam Sekhsaria Postgraduate Scholarship Page](/scholarships/narotam-sekhsaria-postgraduate-scholarship).

## 3. KC Mahindra Scholarship for Post-Graduate Studies Abroad

The KC Mahindra Education Trust awards outright grants rather than loan scholarships. Selected scholars do not need to repay the awarded funds.

The trust awards scholarships annually to ambitious Indian graduates:
- **Top 3 Mahindra Fellows**: **₹10,00,000** grant each.
- **Selected Scholars**: **₹5,00,000** grant each.

Key eligibility requirements:
- **Degree Requirement**: First-class degree or equivalent diploma from a recognized university.
- **Admission Status**: Candidates must hold an admission offer. Alternatively, candidates must have applied to a reputable foreign institution.
- **Interview Location**: Shortlisted applicants must attend final interviews conducted in July.

Check full documentation criteria on the [KC Mahindra Scholarship Page](/scholarships/kc-mahindra-scholarship-for-post-graduate-studies-abroad).

## Combining Trust Scholarships with Education Loans

Most Indian students use a hybrid funding strategy. A single scholarship rarely covers the entire cost of studying abroad.

Students often combine a trust scholarship with a commercial education loan:
1. **Reduce Interest Burden**: Using ₹10,00,000 from an interest-free trust reduces your bank loan principal.
2. **Lower EMI Payments**: A smaller bank loan reduces monthly post-graduation EMI stress. Calculate repayment projections using the [Education Loan EMI Calculator](/tools/education-loan-emi-calculator).
3. **Visa Proof of Funds**: Embassies accept formal trust sanction letters as genuine financial proof.
4. **Remittance Tax Savings**: Comply with Reserve Bank of India Liberalised Remittance Scheme rules. Estimate applicable Tax Collected at Source using the [RBI LRS TCS Calculator](/study-abroad/tools/rbi-lrs-tcs-calculator).

Explore non-collateral and public bank options on the [Study Abroad Loans Guide](/study-abroad#loans).

## Step-by-Step Preparation Checklist

Follow this schedule to submit competitive applications:

1. **Eight Months Prior**: Prepare academic transcripts and request reference letters from senior faculty.
2. **Six Months Prior**: Draft a clear Statement of Purpose outlining research or career goals.
3. **Four Months Prior**: Complete trust registration forms before deadlines expire.
4. **Two Months Prior**: Prepare for technical panel interviews by reviewing core undergraduate coursework.
5. **Final Stage**: Submit university acceptance letters and financial undertaking forms upon selection.

Evaluate personal eligibility for additional state and central schemes using the [Eligibility Checker](/eligibility-checker).
