## What is the FAEA Undergraduate Scholarship?

The Foundation for Academic Excellence and Access (FAEA) offers comprehensive financial aid to undergraduate students in India.

This scheme supports students pursuing first-year undergraduate studies in Arts, Commerce, Science, Engineering, and Professional disciplines across accredited Indian institutions.

FAEA aims to bridge educational disparities by supporting talented students from socially and economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

## Benefits and Financial Assistance Provided

FAEA covers the entire cost of undergraduate education for selected scholars.

The scholarship financial package includes:
- Full tuition fee coverage paid directly to the institution.
- Monthly maintenance allowance for hostel, food, and living expenses.
- Annual book allowance and travel reimbursement.
- Laptop grant or learning device support where applicable.

In addition to financial aid, FAEA conducts national leadership workshops and career readiness sessions for all cohort recipients.

These workshops prepare students for competitive examinations, interviews, and professional corporate careers.

---

## Eligibility Requirements for FAEA Applicants

Applicants must satisfy the following conditions to be considered for selection:
- Must have passed Class 12 from a recognized central or state board in India.
- Must be enrolled in the first year of an undergraduate degree course.
- Must belong to SC, ST, OBC, BPL, or General EWS categories.
- Total annual family income should not exceed ₹3,00,000 per annum.

Students pursuing professional engineering or medical degrees are also eligible to apply.

Academic merit, financial hardship, and leadership potential are evaluated during the candidate selection process.

## How to Apply for FAEA Scholarship Online

Follow these step-by-step instructions to complete your application:

1. **Register on Official FAEA Portal**:
   Visit the official FAEA website and click on the online application link for the current academic cycle.

2. **Fill Personal and Academic Details**:
   Enter Class 12 marks, family income figures, and chosen college course details accurately in the registration portal.

3. **Upload Supporting Documents**:
   Scan and upload your marksheets, caste certificate, income proof, and passport photograph.

4. **Submit Application Before Deadline**:
   Submit the completed form online. Save a printed copy of your application confirmation slip for future reference.

---

## Selection Process and Interview Round

Shortlisted candidates are selected based on academic performance and financial need.

Selected candidates are invited for a regional face-to-face or online interview conducted by an expert panel of educationists.

Final awards are announced after background verification of income certificates and college enrollment proof.
