## 3-Year vs 5-Year LLB: Does the Scholarship Type Change?

Whether you're pursuing the 3-year LLB (after a graduate degree) or the 5-year integrated LLB (directly after Class 12), you qualify for the same category-based central and state post-matric scholarships — the distinction mainly affects which entry-level certificate (graduation or Class 12) you submit at enrollment.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If you belong to a minority community and are in a professional law course, apply under the MCM (Merit-cum-Means) tier rather than the general Post-Matric minority scheme — MCM explicitly covers law degrees with a higher benefit ceiling.

## NLU-Specific Fee Waivers

National Law Universities typically run their own institute-level fee waiver or scholarship programs based on family income, separate from state/central schemes — check with your specific NLU's financial aid or scholarship cell, since these aren't listed on NSP.

---

## Application Tips

1. **Clarify your LLB type (3-year vs 5-year)** on every application form — mismatched course-duration entries are a common rejection reason.
2. **Check your NLU's internal scholarship office** in addition to applying on NSP/state portals.
3. **Renew every semester** with updated mark sheets, since law degrees run on a semester system that doesn't always align with the general academic-year scholarship calendar.
