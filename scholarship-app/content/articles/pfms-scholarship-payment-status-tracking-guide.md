## What is PFMS Scholarship Tracking?

The Public Financial Management System (PFMS) is managed by the Controller General of Accounts, Ministry of Finance.

It tracks and disburses government direct benefit transfer (DBT) funds directly into student bank accounts across India.

Central government schemes, National Scholarship Portal (NSP) awards, and state post-matric scholarships use PFMS for disbursement.

If your scholarship status shows "Payment Sanctioned" or "Approved", the money is routed through PFMS.

## Step-by-Step: How to Check Payment Status by Account Number

Follow these exact steps to track your scholarship payment without entering a password:

1. **Visit Official PFMS Portal**:
   Navigate to the official PFMS portal homepage at pfms.nic.in.

2. **Select 'Know Your Payments'**:
   Click on the **Know Your Payments** or **Track NSP Payments** option on the homepage menu.

3. **Enter Bank Details**:
   Select your bank name from the auto-suggest dropdown list. Enter your full bank account number.

4. **Confirm Account Number and Captcha**:
   Re-enter your account number to prevent typing errors. Enter the security captcha code shown on screen.

5. **Send OTP to Registered Mobile**:
   Click **Send OTP on Registered Mobile Number**. Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your phone.

6. **View Disbursement Status Table**:
   The portal displays transaction date, amount credited, scheme name, and PFMS UTR reference number.

## Tracking Payment Status Using NSP Application ID

If you applied through the National Scholarship Portal, you can track payment directly using your Application ID:

1. Go to the **Track NSP Payment** tab on the PFMS portal.
2. Enter your 14-digit NSP Application ID (for example, UP2025260012345).
3. Enter the captcha code and click **Search**.
4. The system will display the exact stage of your payment processing.

---

## Understanding Common PFMS Status Messages

- **Payment Sanctioned by Agency**:
  The ministry has approved your grant amount. Funds are queued for bank transfer.

- **Under Process with Bank**:
  PFMS has sent the credit instruction to your bank. Money will credit within 24 to 48 hours.

- **Transaction Successful**:
  Funds have been credited into your bank account. Check your bank statement or passbook.

- **Payment Rejected by Bank / Invalid Aadhaar Seeding**:
  The payment failed because your bank account is not mapped with NPCI or is dormant.

---

## What to Do If Payment Is Rejected or Delayed

If your PFMS status displays an error, take immediate corrective action to receive your funds:
- Visit your bank branch and request an **Aadhaar NPCI Seeding Form**.
- Ensure your bank account name matches the name on your Aadhaar card exactly.
- Submit a fresh self-attested Aadhaar copy to activate dormant or inactive bank accounts.
