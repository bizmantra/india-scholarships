## Why Scholarship Applications Get Stuck at the INO Stage

Every online scholarship submission requires formal institutional authentication before reaching state or central welfare departments.

The Institute Nodal Officer (INO) is an appointed faculty member or administrative officer at your college.

Their responsibility involves cross-checking student claims against internal college admission registers, attendance logs, and fee records.

When a dashboard displays "Pending for Verification by Institute Nodal Officer," the application rests in the college portal inbox.

If the institutional verification deadline lapses before the INO acts, the application is discarded automatically by the central server.

Consult our detailed [National Scholarship Portal guide](/guides/nsp) for national verification deadlines and seasonal calendar schedules.

## Step 1: Assemble the Physical Document Dossier

Online submission alone is rarely sufficient for institutional verification. Colleges require hard copies for physical audits.

Print two copies of your submitted online scholarship application form immediately after submitting on the web.

Attach self-attested photocopies of all supporting certificates in the order prescribed by institutional guidelines.

| Document Category | Purpose of INO Verification | Typical Officer Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Admission Slip** | Verifies regular full-time enrollment status | Matches roll number and branch against office records |
| **Academic Marksheet** | Validates qualifying percentage thresholds | Confirms minimum marks without unverified backlogs |
| **College Fee Receipt** | Establishes actual tuition and hostel costs | Prevents inflated or duplicate reimbursement claims |
| **Caste / Income Proof** | Checks eligibility under government quotas | Verifies issuing authority seals and validity dates |

Keep originals handy during submission as officers cross-verify seals on spot before stamping files.

---

## Step 2: Visit the Campus Scholarship Cell in Person

Avoid waiting passively for automated verification updates on student dashboards.

Visit your institution scholarship section or student welfare office during working hours.

Present your physical dossier to the designated assistant or Institute Nodal Officer directly.

Request the officer to locate your application reference number on the institutional portal dashboard.

Officers handle hundreds of candidate files simultaneously; direct presentation expedites file verification before cutoff dates arrive.

Candidates applying for premier central schemes like the [Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students](/scholarships/top-class-education-scheme-for-sc-students) must ensure timely institutional clearance.

---

## Step 3: Handle Verification Errors and Defective Returns

Occasionally an officer marks an application as "Defective" rather than approving or rejecting outright.

A defective status is not a rejection. It provides a temporary correction window for students to rectify discrepancies.

Common defect reasons include mismatched marks entries, illegible fee receipts, or missing parent income renewal certificates.

Log in to the scholarship portal, open the correction module, and update the flagged fields accurately.

Re-upload clean, legible scanned documents and submit the corrected form online.

Submit the revised physical printout to the INO immediately so the officer can validate your file on the portal.

Students in regional universities can review the [Karnataka scholarship hub](/scholarships-in/karnataka) for state-specific administrative procedures.

---

## Escalation Path if Your College Fails to Verify

1. **Meet the Head of Institution**: Present a written representation to your college principal or university registrar.
2. **Contact the District Nodal Officer (DNO)**: Reach out to the district education officer overseeing institutional compliance.
3. **Log a Grievance on NSP**: Submit an official grievance on the central portal citing your application ID and college AISHE code.
