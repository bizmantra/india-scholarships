## Why Dedicated STEM Scholarships for Women Exist

Female enrollment in engineering, technology, and pure sciences remains lower than in other streams, so both the government and corporate CSR foundations run scholarships specifically to close this gap.

These scholarships often carry higher per-student amounts than general category scholarships, and many can be combined with your regular state post-matric scholarship since they come from a different funding source.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Apply for both a STEM-specific CSR scholarship and your state post-matric scheme — check the [/tools/eligibility-checker](/tools/eligibility-checker) to confirm there's no exclusivity clause.

## AICTE Pragati: What You Need to Know

AICTE Pragati is limited to two girl students per family, per year, studying in an AICTE-approved technical institution (engineering, pharmacy, architecture, etc.). It covers tuition fees up to a fixed annual cap and disburses directly via PFMS.

---

## Application Strategy for STEM Applicants

1. **Confirm your institute is AICTE-approved** before applying for AICTE Pragati — non-AICTE colleges are automatically ineligible.
2. **Apply early in the academic year** — CSR programs like Infosys STEM Stars often close applications within a few weeks of opening.
3. **Keep your semester mark sheets ready** — many STEM scholarships require proof of consistent academic performance for renewal.
