The Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship supports up to 5,000 meritorious first-year college students across India every year.

Unlike government Post-Matric schemes that rely entirely on Class 12 cut-offs, Reliance Foundation uses a two-stage evaluation: an online academic application followed by a mandatory, remote-proctored aptitude test.

Selected scholars receive financial support of up to ₹2,00,000 distributed over their degree duration. For complete eligibility thresholds, course lists, and disbursement details, visit our dedicated [Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship details page](/scholarships/reliance-foundation-undergraduate-scholarship).

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Do not wait for the final registration deadline on **October 5, 2026**. The aptitude test link is emailed only after your initial application form is validated, and test slots require completing a system compatibility check beforehand.

---

## Step-by-Step Application Walkthrough

The application process is hosted entirely online at `scholarships.reliancefoundation.org`. Follow this procedural roadmap:

1. **Eligibility Check**:
   Open the official portal, choose "Undergraduate Scholarship", and complete the preliminary questionnaire (regular full-time 1st-year degree enrollment, minimum 60% in Class 12, household income up to ₹15 Lakhs).

2. **Account Registration**:
   Register using your personal email address and mobile number. Ensure this number remains active, as test links, slot confirmations, and OTPs are delivered via SMS and email.

3. **Complete Educational and Personal Details**:
   Enter personal information, Class 10 and 12 percentages, current college affiliation, and degree programme (B.A., B.Com., B.Sc., B.Tech., MBBS, LLB, or other recognized full-time undergraduate degrees).

4. **Upload Digitized Documents**:
   Attach clear PDF or JPEG scans of your academic certificates, college enrollment proof, and income documentation (keep files under 2 MB each).

5. **Submit the Application**:
   Review all fields carefully before submitting. Information cannot be edited once finalized.

6. **Take the Practice Test**:
   Reliance Foundation sends a system check link approximately one week before your test slot. Complete this practice run to confirm camera, microphone, and browser compatibility.

7. **Appear for the Proctored Aptitude Test**:
   Log in at your assigned slot to complete the 60-minute test. Skipping this test results in immediate disqualification.

## Aptitude Test: Pattern, Syllabus & Strategy

The aptitude test evaluates foundational reasoning, numeracy, and English comprehension rather than university subject curricula.

| Test Parameter | Specification | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 60 minutes | Timed countdown; auto-submits on completion |
| **Questions** | 60 MCQs | 1 minute per question average |
| **Test Mode** | Online, Remote Proctored | Webcam, microphone, and full-screen proctoring |
| **Sections** | 3 Modules | Verbal Ability, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, Numerical Ability |

### Syllabus Breakdown

- **Verbal Ability (20 Questions)**: Assesses grammar, vocabulary, sentence completion, reading comprehension, analogies, and error spotting.
- **Analytical & Logical Reasoning (20 Questions)**: Focuses on coding-decoding, number/letter series, direction sense, blood relations, and syllogisms.
- **Numerical Ability (20 Questions)**: Class 10 arithmetic including percentages, ratios, averages, profit and loss, time and work, and basic data tables.

## Hardware and System Requirements

The test is remote-proctored, with camera, microphone and screen-sharing turned on. Check the exact device requirements in your test email, and plan for these basics:

- **Device**: A laptop or desktop is the safest choice. Screen-sharing and proctoring often do not work properly on phones.
- **Webcam and Microphone**: Both must stay on throughout the test.
- **Stable Internet**: Connect via broadband, Wi-Fi, or LAN cable. Mobile hotspot tethering frequently drops during video proctoring.
- **Browser**: Use updated Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge with ad-blockers and extensions disabled.

## Required Documents & Verification Standards

Prepare these official documents before starting your submission:

1. **Class 12 Marksheet**: Original marksheet showing at least 60% aggregate.
2. **Class 10 Passing Certificate**: For age and name verification.
3. **Bonafide Student Certificate**: Issued on college letterhead signed by the Dean, Principal, or Registrar, or an official first-year admission fee receipt.
4. **Valid Household Income Proof**:
   - Government revenue certificate signed by an Executive Magistrate, Tehsildar, or Revenue Officer.
   - For salaried parents: Latest Form 16 or Income Tax Return (ITR-V) acknowledgement.
5. **Government Photo ID**: Aadhaar card, Voter ID, or Passport.

## 5 Fatal Mistakes That Cause Rejection

1. **Skipping the System Check**: If your camera, microphone or screen-sharing fails during the test, you may not get another attempt.
2. **Missing the Scheduled Slot**: Slots cannot be rescheduled once missed without verified advance notice.
3. **Tab Switching**: Navigating outside the exam window or connecting a second monitor triggers an automatic test lock.
4. **Invalid Income Certificates**: Income proof must be issued by a relevant authority. Expired or unofficial documents can get your application rejected.
5. **Waiting Until October 5**: High portal load on the closing date can delay OTP generation and document uploads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can second or third-year college students apply?
No. The scholarship is restricted to students enrolled in the **first year** of a regular full-time undergraduate degree.

### Is there any registration or test fee?
No. Reliance Foundation never charges any application or examination fee.

### Can I receive a government scholarship simultaneously?
Yes. Reliance Foundation permits holding government Post-Matric scholarships, provided your college and the government scheme permit holding both awards.

### How is the final merit list determined?
Final selection combines Class 12 marks, aptitude test scores, and socioeconomic background, giving priority to households earning below ₹2.5 Lakhs annually.
