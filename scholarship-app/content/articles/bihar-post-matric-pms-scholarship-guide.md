> [!NOTE]
> **Key Takeaways for Bihar Post-Matric PMS 2026-27**:
> - The official Bihar PMS portal (`pmsonline.bih.nic.in`) accepts online applications for Backward Class (BC), Extremely Backward Class (EBC), Scheduled Caste (SC), and Scheduled Tribe (ST) students.
> - Full tuition fee reimbursement up to ₹45,000 per annum is available for approved post-secondary institutions inside and outside Bihar.
> - Mandatory document verification is conducted at both institute and District Nodal Officer (DNO) levels before Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) credit.

The Education Department of Bihar provides post-matric financial assistance to eligible students pursuing ITI, Polytechnic, General Degree (BA, BSc, BCom), Professional Courses (BTech, MBBS, BBA, BCA), and Postgraduate studies. Understanding registration criteria, income certificates, and bonafide documentation ensures smooth approval.

## Scheme Eligibility Criteria & Overview

Eligibility rules established by the Government of Bihar include:

| Parameter | BC / EBC Category Scheme | SC / ST Category Scheme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domicile** | Permanent Resident of Bihar | Permanent Resident of Bihar |
| **Annual Family Income** | Up to ₹3,000,000 per annum | Up to ₹3,000,000 per annum |
| **Academic Level** | Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Diploma, UG, PG | Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Diploma, UG, PG |

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

Follow this structured workflow on the official Bihar PMS Online portal:

### Step 1: Student Registration
1. Access the portal at `https://pmsonline.bih.nic.in`.
2. Select your category link: **BC / EBC Student Registration** or **SC / ST Student Registration**.
3. Read self-declaration instructions and check all mandatory agreement boxes.
4. Enter student name, father's name, DOB, Aadhaar number, and mobile number.
5. Verify OTP sent to mobile and email to complete registration.

### Step 2: User ID & Password Activation
1. Upon preliminary verification of Aadhaar and bank details by the department system, your User ID and Password will be sent via SMS within 2–3 working days.
2. Log in using your received credentials and change your default password.

### Step 3: Application Submission & Document Upload
1. Fill in academic details, college selection, AISHE/DISE code, and course year.
2. Upload scanned copies of the following valid documents (PDF under 400 KB):
   - Bihar Residence Certificate (issued by CO/SDO/DM)
   - Income Certificate (issued for financial year 2026–27)
   - Caste Certificate
   - Official College Bonafide Certificate (with fee breakdown)
   - Previous Year Marksheet

### Step 4: Final Verification & Tracking
1. Review filled details and submit the application online.
2. Download and print the acknowledgment slip.
3. Submit physical copies of all documents to your institute scholarship counter.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What to Do If Bihar Out-of-State College Is Not Listed?
If studying at an accredited institution outside Bihar, request your institute nodal officer to register on `pmsonline.bih.nic.in` under the **Out of State Institution Registration** tab.

### 2. Is Bonafide Certificate Mandatory for Bihar PMS?
Yes, a valid Bonafide Student Certificate specifying your current academic session and non-refundable fee structure is mandatory.

### 3. How Can Applicants Track Application Status Online?
Visit `pmsonline.bih.nic.in`, click on **Verify Your Application Status**, and enter your Application ID or Aadhaar number to view physical verification and bank clearance updates.
