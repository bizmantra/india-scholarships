## Overview of Medical & MBBS Scholarships

Medical education in India requires substantial financial investment across five years of study.

While government medical college tuition is subsidized, hostel fees, books, clinical equipment, and private college tuition can stretch family budgets.

Both government state portals and corporate trust foundations fund MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, and Allied Health Science students.

Discover matching opportunities for your medical stream using our [Eligibility Checker](/eligibility-checker).

## 1. Central and State Post-Matric Medical Scholarships

State Social Welfare Departments provide 100% fee reimbursement or annual stipends for SC, ST, OBC, and EWS medical students.

Key Features:
- **Tuition Fee Coverage**: Government reimburses full tuition fees for government seats in medical colleges.
- **Maintenance Allowance**: Monthly hostel and living allowance paid directly via direct benefit transfer (DBT).
- **Eligibility**: Domicile of respective state with family income below state category thresholds (usually ≤ ₹2.5 Lakh to ₹6 Lakh).

> 💡 **Government Quota vs Management/NRI Quota**: State fee-reimbursement schemes only apply to students admitted under the government quota at government or government-aided colleges — they do not cover management or NRI quota seats, which can cost significantly more per year. If you're on a management-quota seat at a private college, the private CSR grants below are your main funding route.

---

## 2. Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship for Medical Students

The Sitaram Jindal Foundation provides one of the largest private financial grants for MBBS and BDS students nationwide.

Read full details on our [Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship Page](/scholarships/sitaram-jindal-foundation-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Monthly Stipend**: Up to ₹3,000 per month (₹36,000 per year) for male MBBS students.
- **Enhanced Girl Student Allowance**: Up to ₹3,300 per month (₹39,600 per year) for female MBBS students.
- **Eligibility**: Minimum 70% in Class 12 for boys (65% for girls); family income ≤ ₹4,00,000.

---

## 3. Tata Trusts Medical and Healthcare Performance Grants

Tata Trusts provides financial assistance to undergraduate and postgraduate medical students across India.

It supports students pursuing MBBS, BDS, Master of Public Health (MPH), and Master of Hospital Administration (MHA).

Key Highlights:
- **Grant Coverage**: Partial tuition fee waiver paid directly to accredited medical institutions.
- **Selection Criteria**: Academic merit in previous professional year and verified family financial need.

---

## 4. Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship (Medical Stream)

Meritorious first-year MBBS students enrolled in recognized Indian medical colleges can apply for the Reliance Foundation grant.

View full application details on our [Reliance Foundation Scholarship Page](/scholarships/reliance-foundation-undergraduate-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Grant Amount**: Up to ₹2,00,000 over the course of study.
- **Eligibility**: First-year undergraduate medical student with 60%+ in Class 12; family income ≤ ₹6,00,000.

## Important Tips for Medical Applicants

- **Submit Fee Structure Copy**: Obtain an official itemized fee breakdown letter signed by your college Dean or Principal.
- **Apply Early**: Submit private trust forms in the first semester of your MBBS course.
- **Keep Bonafide Certificate Ready**: Maintain a fresh Bonafide Student Certificate issued by your medical college.
- **Prove NEET-Based Admission**: Most schemes require your NEET scorecard alongside your admission letter to confirm you were admitted through the standard entrance process, not a management-quota bypass.
- **Renew Every Academic Year**: Medical scholarships are rarely one-time awards — resubmit updated semester results and income proof annually.
