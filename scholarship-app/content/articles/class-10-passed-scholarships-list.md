## Why Class 10 Pass is a Scholarship Turning Point

Passing Class 10 shifts you from Pre-Matric to Post-Matric scholarship eligibility — Post-Matric schemes generally offer higher benefit amounts and cover both tuition and maintenance, unlike most Pre-Matric schemes which only cover a small stipend.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Apply for Post-Matric registration on NSP or your state portal within the first few weeks after your Class 10 result — late registration can cause you to miss an entire year's benefit cycle.

---

## Top Schemes to Apply for Right After Class 10

| Scholarship | What It Covers | Who It's For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [PM Yashasvi Scholarship](/scholarships/pm-yashasvi-scholarship) | Entrance-exam based scholarship for Class 11-12 and beyond | OBC, EBC, DNT students |
| [AICTE Swanath Scholarship Scheme](/scholarships/aicte-swanath-scholarship-scheme) | Support for orphans and specially-abled students entering technical education | Orphans, differently-abled students |

---

## Choosing Between Academic Streams

If you're moving into Class 11 Science, Commerce, or Arts, most Post-Matric category-based scholarships (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Minority) apply regardless of stream — but course-specific scholarships like AICTE Swanath only apply if you eventually pursue a technical/diploma track.

---

## Application Checklist

1. **Register on NSP as a new Post-Matric applicant** immediately after your Class 10 result, don't wait for Class 11 admission to complete.
2. **Update your caste/income certificate** if your previous one was issued for Pre-Matric purposes — some states require a fresh certificate at this transition point.
3. **Track the PM Yashasvi exam calendar** if you belong to an eligible category — the entrance exam is typically held once a year.
