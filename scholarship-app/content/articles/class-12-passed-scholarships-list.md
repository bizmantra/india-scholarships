## Class 12 Pass: The Widest Scholarship Window

Passing Class 12 opens the door to the largest pool of scholarships in the Indian system — UG degree post-matric schemes, merit scholarships tied to board results, and entrance-exam-based scholarships for professional courses all become available simultaneously.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Apply to your board-result-based scholarships and your entrance-exam-based scholarships separately — they run through different portals and have different deadlines.

---

## Top Class 12-Triggered Scholarships

| Scholarship | Basis | Course Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [BITSAT Scholarship](/scholarships/bitsat-scholarship) | Entrance exam score (BITSAT) | Engineering at BITS campuses |
| [Central Sector Scholarship (Kerala applicants via DCE)](/scholarships/central-sector-scholarship-kerala-applicants-via-dce) | Class 12 board result | Any regular UG degree |
| [Aditya Birla Scholarship Programme](/scholarships/aditya-birla-scholarship-programme) | Merit + means | Engineering, Management, and other professional courses |

---

## Board Result vs Entrance Exam Scholarships

Board-result scholarships (like the Central Sector Scholarship) are typically awarded based on your Class 12 percentile within your state board, and don't require any additional exam. Entrance-exam scholarships (like BITSAT) require you to have appeared for and scored well in a specific competitive exam — passing Class 12 alone isn't sufficient.

---

## Application Checklist

1. **Register on NSP immediately** after your Class 12 result to start your Post-Matric UG application.
2. **Separately apply for entrance-exam scholarships** as soon as your exam results and admission are confirmed.
3. **Renew your caste/income certificate** for the new academic year — Class 10-era certificates typically need refreshing.
