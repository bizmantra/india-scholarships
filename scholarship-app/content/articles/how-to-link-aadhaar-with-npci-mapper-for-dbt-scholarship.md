## Step 1: Understand the Difference Between Linking and Seeding

A common misunderstanding among scholarship applicants is assuming that standard bank Aadhaar linking completes DBT readiness.

Standard linking connects Aadhaar to your bank record for KYC compliance. It allows biometric withdrawals and account verification.

In contrast, NPCI Aadhaar seeding maps your 12-digit Aadhaar number on the central NPCI mapper. The government uses this routing protocol for all direct benefit transfers.

When the government issues scholarship disbursements through the Public Financial Management System, the payment gateway pings NPCI servers. Funds fail to credit if active NPCI mapping is absent.

Review the [National Scholarship Portal guide](/guides/nsp) to verify your portal profile settings before submitting renewal applications.

---

## Step 2: Check Your Live Seeding Status Online

Students can verify their current NPCI seeding status without visiting bank branches.

Visit the official UIDAI resident portal at `myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in`. Log in with your Aadhaar number and the OTP received on your linked mobile phone.

Navigate to the Bank Seeding Status dashboard module. The screen displays the name of the bank mapped to your Aadhaar.

| Status Displayed | Operational Meaning | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active** | Bank account is mapped to NPCI server | No action required; ready for DBT credit |
| **Inactive** | Former mapping expired or was decoupled | Visit bank immediately with consent form |
| **No Record Found** | Aadhaar has never been mapped on NPCI | Submit fresh seeding request at branch |

If your status reads Inactive, scholarship disbursements like the [UP Scholarship Portal scheme](/scholarships/up-scholarship-portal) cannot process financial transfers.

---

## Step 3: Complete Offline NPCI Seeding at Your Bank Branch

When online status displays Inactive or points to a dormant bank account, offline submission is required.

Download the standard "Application for Linking / Seeding Aadhaar Number and Receiving DBT Benefits" form. Most public sector banks provide this form at help desks.

Fill in your active savings account number and 12-digit Aadhaar number. Tick Option 1 to mandate primary DBT credits to this specific account.

Hand over the completed form alongside a self-attested Aadhaar photocopy and bank passbook copy to the branch customer service officer.

Request the teller to enter the transaction on the bank core banking system. Ask for an acknowledgment slip containing the Service Request Number.

Official bank processing usually takes 48 to 72 business hours to reflect on central NPCI servers.

---

## Step 4: Verify PFMS Payment Gateway Readiness

After bank processing concludes, verify whether central payment systems detect your updated configuration.

Central schemes such as the [Post-Matric Scholarships in India](/scholarships/post-matric-scholarships-india) execute automated database validations weekly.

Log in to the PFMS "Track DBT Details" portal. Choose the relevant scholarship category and provide your registration number.

Ensure the Beneficiary Status line displays "Validated" and Account Status shows "Aadhaar Seeded."

Students exploring funding in other regions can check the [Uttar Pradesh scholarship hub](/scholarships-in/uttar-pradesh) for state-specific verification timelines.

---

## Key Mistakes to Avoid During Aadhaar Seeding

1. **Multiple Accounts Confusion**: An individual can possess multiple bank accounts, but NPCI maps only one account at any moment.
2. **Minor Account Restrictions**: Accounts opened as minor accounts often carry transaction caps that block scholarship tranches over ₹50,000.
3. **Dormant Account Inactivity**: Accounts without customer-induced activity for six months freeze automatic DBT deposits.
