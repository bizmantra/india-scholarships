## What is the Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)?

PMSS is a dedicated scholarship for the wards and widows of Army, Navy, Air Force, and Central Armed Police Force (CAPF) personnel — including those killed or disabled in action, as well as regularly retired personnel.

Unlike most other central schemes, PMSS does not apply a family income ceiling. Eligibility is based purely on the serving or ex-serving status of the parent, making it accessible even to middle-income defence families.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: PMSS applications are processed through the Kendriya Sainik Board / Zila Sainik Board network, not the general NSP portal — start your application at your local Sainik Welfare office.

---

## Eligibility Snapshot

| Criteria | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Parent's Status** | Ex-servicemen, serving personnel, or war widow/widower of Army, Navy, Air Force, CAPF |
| **Course** | Professional degree courses (Engineering, Medical, MBA, MCA, Nursing, etc.) |
| **Minimum Marks** | 60% in Class 12 (typically; varies by course category) |
| **Income Ceiling** | None |

---

## Application Process

1. **Register with your local Zila Sainik Board** and obtain a certificate confirming your parent's ex-servicemen/serving status.
2. **Apply via the Kendriya Sainik Board (KSB) online portal**, uploading your Class 12 mark sheet and admission proof.
3. **Track disbursement** through KSB's own status-check system, which is separate from NSP/PFMS.
