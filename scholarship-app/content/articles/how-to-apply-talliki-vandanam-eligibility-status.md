## What is the AP Talliki Vandanam Scheme?

The Talliki Vandanam Scheme is an education welfare initiative in Andhra Pradesh.
The scheme supports mothers from low-income families.
It encourages them to send their children to school regularly.

The government provides a financial benefit of ₹15,000 per year.
This applies to every school-going child in the household.
Unlike the older scheme, the benefit is not restricted to one child.

Out of the ₹15,000, the school deducts ₹2,000 for building maintenance.
The remaining ₹13,000 is credited directly to the mother's bank account.
This direct benefit transfer helps families manage school costs easily.

## Eligibility Criteria for Talliki Vandanam

Applicants must meet specific conditions to qualify for the scheme.
The student must be a permanent resident of Andhra Pradesh.
They must study in a recognized school in the state.

The scheme covers students from Class 1 to Class 12.
This includes government, aided, and private schools.
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% school attendance.

The family must possess a valid Rice Card.
Family income must be below the rural and urban limits.
The rural limit is ₹10,000 per month.
The urban limit is ₹12,000 per month.

Eligible landholding is under 3 acres wet or 10 acres dry land.
Family electricity consumption must be below 300 units monthly.
Government employees and income tax payers are excluded.

## Required Documents for Application

Mothers must submit relevant documents to the school authorities.
Make sure all documents have matching name spellings.
Keep scanned copies ready for online verification.

The student needs a valid Aadhaar Card.
The mother must also submit her Aadhaar Card.
An active Rice Card is mandatory for verification.

Provide the school admission receipt and student ID.
Submit the mother's active bank account passbook.
The bank account must have active NPCI Aadhaar mapping.

## Step-by-Step Status Verification Guide

Follow these steps to check the application and payment status.
Keep the mother's Aadhaar number ready before starting.

### Step 1: Visit the Official Portal
Open the official Beneficiary Management website.
The URL is [gsws-nbm.ap.gov.in](https://gsws-nbm.ap.gov.in/BM/).

### Step 2: Select Scheme Details
Navigate to the application status section.
Choose the scheme name as Talliki Vandanam.
Select the current academic year 2026-2027.

### Step 3: Enter Aadhaar Number
Enter the mother's Aadhaar card number.
Click the search button to load the details.

### Step 4: Review Payment Status
The screen shows the current verification status.
It displays if the payment is released or pending.
It shows if any biometric verification is incomplete.

## How to Register a Grievance

Mothers can file a complaint if payments are delayed.
The grievance window is open at local secretariats.

Visit your nearest Village or Ward Secretariat office.
Request the welfare assistant to check the GSWS portal.
They can trace why the payment did not credit.

Common issues include missing biometric e-KYC records.
Another issue is inactive Aadhaar bank account mapping.
Resolve these errors at the secretariat to receive funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can one family get benefits for multiple children?
Yes, the scheme pays ₹15,000 per child for all school-going children.

### Q2: What is the attendance requirement?
The student must maintain at least 75% attendance in school.

### Q3: How is the money sent to beneficiaries?
The net amount of ₹13,000 is sent via DBT to the mother's account.
