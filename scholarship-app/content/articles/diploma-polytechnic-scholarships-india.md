## Diploma Students Are Often Underserved by Scholarship Awareness

Diploma and polytechnic students are eligible for nearly all the same government post-matric scholarships as degree students, but awareness is lower because most scholarship marketing focuses on "college" or "university" students rather than technical institutes.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Check both your caste/category-based state scholarship AND any diploma-specific top-up scheme run by your state's Directorate of Technical Education — they are usually separate applications.

## Lateral Entry Students: What Changes

If you're moving from a diploma to a B.Tech second-year seat via lateral entry, you must update your scholarship application to reflect your new course level and institute — continuing on your old diploma-level application will cause your renewal to be rejected during verification.

---

## Quick Application Checklist

1. **Confirm your polytechnic is AICTE-approved** — several technical scholarships require this.
2. **Reapply each semester**, not just once at admission, since diploma schemes are usually semester-wise rather than annual.
3. **Check for ITI-to-diploma bridge scholarships** if you completed an ITI course before your diploma.
