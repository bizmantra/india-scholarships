## The Three Tiers of Minority Scholarships

The Ministry of Minority Affairs (MoMA) scheme, delivered through the National Scholarship Portal, has three distinct tiers, and applicants often apply to the wrong one:

1. **Pre-Matric Scholarship**: For minority students in Class 1–10.
2. **Post-Matric Scholarship**: For minority students in Class 11 and above, including degree and diploma courses.
3. **Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship**: Reserved specifically for professional and technical degree/diploma courses like engineering, medicine, and law.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If you're in a professional course, always apply under MCM first — it typically offers a higher benefit amount than the general Post-Matric tier.

---

## Eligible Communities & Income Limits

| Tier | Income Ceiling | Course Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Matric** | ₹1 Lakh per annum | Class 1–10 |
| **Post-Matric** | ₹2 Lakh per annum | Class 11 onwards |
| **MCM** | ₹2.5 Lakh per annum | Professional/Technical only |

Eligible communities are Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, and Parsi (Zoroastrian), as notified by the Central Government from time to time.

## Documents & Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Community declaration mismatch**: Ensure your self-declaration form lists the exact community name as per the central notified list.
2. **Wrong tier selection**: Applying for general Post-Matric instead of MCM for a professional course results in a lower benefit or rejection during institute verification.
3. **Bank seeding issues**: MoMA scholarships strictly disburse through PFMS — an unseeded Aadhaar account will delay your payment even after approval.
