## Commerce Students Often Overlook Merit-Based CSR Scholarships

Because most scholarship marketing centers on engineering and medical courses, B.Com and BBA students often assume they're ineligible for corporate CSR scholarships — but many of these programs are open to any UG stream, and finance-sector foundations actively prefer commerce students.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Search for "general degree" or "any stream" wording in scholarship eligibility criteria — commerce students qualify for far more schemes than the course-specific ones suggest.

## Central Sector Scholarship for Commerce Students

The Central Sector Scholarship Scheme rewards top-performing Class 12 board students pursuing regular undergraduate degree courses — B.Com and BBA both qualify, provided you're enrolled in a full-time regular course (not correspondence/distance learning).

---

## Application Tips

1. **Don't skip "general" merit scholarships** just because they don't name commerce explicitly — read the full eligibility clause.
2. **Highlight extracurriculars** for CSR scholarships from finance-sector companies — several weigh leadership and communication skills alongside marks.
3. **Apply for renewal with each semester's results**, as most commerce scholarships require continuous academic performance tracking.
