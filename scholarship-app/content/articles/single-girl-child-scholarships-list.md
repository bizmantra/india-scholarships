## What Makes "Single Girl Child" Scholarships Different?

Unlike general "Girls" or "Female" scholarships that simply require the applicant to be female, Single Girl Child schemes add a stricter condition: the applicant must be the only child, or the only daughter with no brothers, in her family.

This distinction matters because it determines which affidavit format you need — a general girls' scholarship needs no such proof, but Single Girl Child schemes reject applications without a notarized parental affidavit.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Check both your school and state welfare department for the exact affidavit template — formats differ slightly by state and issuing board.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

| Criteria | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Family Structure** | Must be the only child, or only daughter with no living brothers |
| **Academic Requirement** | Typically 60%+ in the qualifying exam (varies by scheme) |
| **Affidavit** | Notarized, on stamp paper, signed by parent/guardian |
| **Course Level** | Class 11 onwards through undergraduate degree |

## State & Central Options

- **Maharashtra**: The [Maharashtra Girls Merit Scholarship](/scholarships/maharashtra-girls-merit) rewards high-scoring female students, with additional weightage for single girl child applicants.
- **Odisha**: [Sudakshya for Girls Child Scholarship](/scholarships/sudakshya-for-girls-child-scholarship-odisha) is specifically structured around this eligibility condition.
- **Bihar**: [Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana](/scholarships/mukhyamantri-kanya-utthan-yojana-graduation) supports girl students through to graduation, with incentive payments at each academic milestone.

---

## How to Get the Affidavit Right

1. **Draft on non-judicial stamp paper** of the value specified by your state (usually ₹10–100).
2. **Get it notarized** by a registered notary — a plain self-declaration without notarization is commonly rejected.
3. **Match names exactly** with Aadhaar and mark sheet — spelling mismatches are the most common rejection cause.
