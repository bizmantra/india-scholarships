## What is the SC Post-Matric Scholarship?

The SC Post-Matric Scholarship is a central sector scheme that supports Scheduled Caste students studying beyond Class 10, covering tuition fees, maintenance allowance, and other compulsory charges.

It is delivered as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, with the central government and state governments sharing the funding, and disbursement happens through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or the equivalent state SC welfare portal.

## Income Ceiling and Free-Ship Rules

The parental/family income ceiling for the central SC Post-Matric Scholarship is ₹2.5 Lakh per annum. Students whose family income is below this limit qualify for full fee reimbursement under what is commonly called the "Free-Ship" or fee-reimbursement component.

| Component | Coverage | Income Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tuition Fee Reimbursement** | Full course fee as per state-notified fee structure | Income under ₹2.5 Lakh |
| **Maintenance Allowance** | Monthly stipend for hostellers/day-scholars | Income under ₹2.5 Lakh |
| **Non-Refundable Fees** | Exam fee, library fee, lab fee | Reimbursed alongside tuition |
| **State Top-Up Schemes** | Additional stipend or book grants | Varies by state (some raise the cap) |

## How States Vary the SC Scholarship Rules

While the central scheme sets a ₹2.5 Lakh baseline, several states run parallel or supplementary schemes with their own limits and disbursement timelines:

- **Telangana**: TS ePASS disburses SC Post-Matric benefits directly via [/scholarships/telangana-postmatric-sc](/scholarships/telangana-postmatric-sc), with income proof cross-verified against ration card data.
- **Andhra Pradesh**: The AP SC Post-Matric Scholarship at [/scholarships/andhra-pradesh-postmatric-sc](/scholarships/andhra-pradesh-postmatric-sc) is bundled with Jagananna Vidya Deevena for fee reimbursement.
- **Tamil Nadu**: A combined SC/ST Post-Matric Scholarship at [/scholarships/tamil-nadu-post-matric-scholarship-for-scst](/scholarships/tamil-nadu-post-matric-scholarship-for-scst) adds a hostel allowance on top of the central benefit.

## Documents Required for Application

1. **Caste Certificate**: Issued by the Tehsildar or competent Revenue Officer, listing the applicant as Scheduled Caste.
2. **Income Certificate**: Must be dated within the current financial year and issued by an authorized officer.
3. **Bonafide/Admission Proof**: Confirms enrollment in a recognized post-matric course.
4. **Bank Passbook**: Aadhaar-seeded account for direct benefit transfer (DBT) via PFMS.

---

## Common Rejection Reasons and Fixes

Applications are frequently rejected for income certificates issued in the wrong financial year, caste certificates that don't match the applicant's name exactly as per Aadhaar, or bank accounts not seeded with Aadhaar under NPCI mapping.

Before final submission, cross-check your name spelling across all uploaded documents and confirm your bank seeding status on the UIDAI portal.
