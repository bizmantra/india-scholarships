The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) is run by the Ministry of Education to stop bright students from dropping out after Class 8.

Each year 1,00,000 fresh scholarships are awarded across India. Selected students get ₹12,000 a year from Class 9 to Class 12.

This guide covers how to actually get it. For state quotas and scheme details, see the [Karnataka NMMS page](/scholarships/nmms-scholarship-karnataka).

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Passing the state exam does not pay you anything by itself. You must also register on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) after you are selected.

---

## Who Can Take the NMMS Exam?

- **Class**: studying in Class 8 in a government, local body or government-aided school.
- **Not eligible**: students of Kendriya Vidyalayas, Navodaya Vidyalayas and private unaided schools.
- **Class 7 marks**: at least 55% (50% for SC/ST).
- **Family income**: not more than ₹3,50,000 a year from all sources.

Each state gets a fixed number of seats, and students are ranked within their state.

---

## Where to Register

Students do not apply on a central website for the state exam. Your school headmaster submits your details on the state board's portal.

| State | Conducting body | Official site |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Karnataka** | KSEAB | `kseab.karnataka.gov.in` |
| **Maharashtra** | MSCE Pune | `mscenmms.in` |
| **Gujarat** | State Examination Board | `sebexam.org` |

See our pages for [Maharashtra NMMS](/scholarships/nmms-scholarship-maharashtra), [Gujarat NMMS](/scholarships/gujarat-national-means-cum-merit-scholarship-nmms) and the [national scheme overview](/scholarships/national-means-cum-merit-scholarship).

## Exam Pattern and Pass Marks

The test is on paper, with two papers on the same day:

| Paper | What it tests | Questions | Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MAT** | Reasoning, analogy, classification, patterns | 90 | 90 |
| **SAT** | Science, Social Science, Maths (Class 7 and 8 syllabus) | 90 | 90 |

To qualify you must score at least **40% in aggregate across both papers** (**32% for SC/ST**). Passing the cut-off does not guarantee a scholarship, because seats are limited by state and category quota.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Give your documents to the school.** Hand your Class 7 marksheet, income certificate and category certificate to your headmaster. The school registers you on the state portal.
2. **Collect your admit card** from the school before the exam.
3. **Take the exam.** Carry your admit card and school ID.
4. **Check the result** on your state board's website.
5. **Register on NSP.** Selected students apply on `scholarships.gov.in`. Follow our [NSP OTR registration guide](/guides/nsp-otr-one-time-registration-guide-2026).
6. **Renew every year** on NSP.

---

## How the Money Reaches You

The ministry pays through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the student's bank account. The account should be in the student's own name and linked to Aadhaar for DBT. If DBT fails, check your Aadhaar-bank link using our [NPCI mapper guide](/guides/how-to-link-aadhaar-with-npci-mapper-for-dbt-scholarship).

## Documents Checklist

- Class 7 marksheet (55% / 50% for SC/ST)
- Income certificate from the revenue authority
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC)
- Disability certificate (PwD quota)
- Aadhaar card matching school records
- Bank passbook copy

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Waiting for the school.** Ask your headmaster for the state's last date early. Schools register all students in one go.
2. **Wrong income certificate.** Use a certificate from the revenue authority, not a self-declaration.
3. **Not registering on NSP after you qualify.** No registration means no payment.
4. **Name or Aadhaar mismatch** between school records, Aadhaar and bank account.
5. **Failing a class.** The scholarship continues only if you pass each year with the required marks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Navodaya or Kendriya Vidyalaya students apply?
No. Only government, local body and government-aided school students are eligible.

### What marks are needed to keep the scholarship?
You must pass each class with the required marks. Check the current renewal rules on NSP before each year starts.

### Is it ₹1,000 a month or ₹12,000 a year?
The scheme value is ₹12,000 a year (₹1,000 a month). Payment timing depends on how NSP releases the DBT.

### Can a student apply directly without the school?
No. For the state exam, your school submits your application.
