## Why Apply for Corporate CSR Scholarships?

Engineering and diploma courses require significant expenditure on tuition fees, hostel accommodation, and project equipment.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) foundation grants provide financial relief to meritorious engineering and polytechnic students.

These scholarships are funded by top Indian corporate entities under India's Companies Act mandatory CSR allocations.

Most CSR scholarships are awarded based on academic merit combined with financial need.

Check your complete eligibility across technical courses on our [Eligibility Checker](/eligibility-checker).

---

## 1. Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship

The Reliance Foundation offers 5,000 undergraduate scholarships annually to meritorious students across India.

Engineering, computer science, and technology stream students are strongly encouraged to apply.

View full application details on our [Reliance Foundation Scholarship Page](/scholarships/reliance-foundation-undergraduate-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Scholarship Amount**: Up to ₹2,00,000 over the duration of the degree course.
- **Eligibility**: Enrolled in 1st year full-time undergraduate degree with minimum 60% in Class 12.
- **Family Income Limit**: Preference given to annual family income under ₹2,50,000 per annum.

---

## 2. Tata Capital Pankh Scholarship Scheme

The Tata Capital Pankh Scholarship supports underprivileged students pursuing professional undergraduate and diploma courses.

It provides targeted financial grants for tuition fee reimbursement.

Read full guidelines on our [Tata Capital Pankh Scholarship Page](/scholarships/tata-capital-pankh-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Scholarship Amount**: Up to ₹50,000 or 80% of annual course fees for B.Tech students.
- **Diploma Grant**: Up to ₹15,000 for polytechnic diploma students.
- **Eligibility**: Minimum 60% marks in previous academic year; family income ≤ ₹4,00,000.

---

## 3. Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship

The Sitaram Jindal Foundation provides recurring monthly stipends to engineering and polytechnic diploma students nationwide.

This scheme operates throughout the year with rolling application acceptance.

Explore complete details on our [Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship Page](/scholarships/sitaram-jindal-foundation-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Stipend Amount**: ₹2,00,000+ total cumulative support; monthly grants paid directly to students.
- **Eligibility**: Diploma engineering students (60% marks for boys, 55% for girls); B.Tech students (70% for boys, 65% for girls).

---

## 4. HDFC Bank Parivartan's ECSS Scholarship

HDFC Bank's Educational Crisis Support Scholarship (ECSS) assists students facing financial hardship or personal crisis.

It supports merit-cum-means technical and general undergraduate course enrollment.

Read full details on our [HDFC Bank Parivartan ECSS Page](/scholarships/hdfc-bank-parivartan-ecss-scholarship).

Key Highlights:
- **Scholarship Amount**: Up to ₹75,000 per year for professional undergraduate streams.
- **Eligibility**: Minimum 55% in previous examination; family income ≤ ₹6,00,000.

---

## Key Application Tips for Engineering Students

- **Keep Marksheets Organized**: Ensure semester marksheets are attested by your college registrar or HOD.
- **Submit Income Certificate**: Obtain a valid income certificate from your local revenue authority.
- **Track Deadlines**: Most corporate foundation portals open between July and November each year.
