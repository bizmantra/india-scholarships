## Understanding the Digital Gujarat Portal

The Digital Gujarat Portal is the unified platform for state scholarships.

It manages applications, online verification, and final disbursements.

Students in Gujarat get financial aid directly into bank accounts.

> 💡 **Official Tip**: The official URL is digitalgujarat.gov.in. Registration on this portal is completely free.

---

The platform hosts multiple financial schemes for various student categories.

1. **For SC Students**: Apply for the [Digital Gujarat Post-Matric SC Scholarship](/scholarships/digital-gujarat-post-matric-scholarship-for-sc-students) program.

## Eligibility Criteria for Applicants

Students must fulfill specific conditions to receive funding.

### 1. Domicile Requirement
The applicant must reside permanently in Gujarat. 

Students from other states cannot apply on this portal.

### 2. Family Income Limits
The income limit varies across category rules.

For OBC/SEBC students, the limit is ₹2.5 Lakhs.

For SC/ST students, the limit is also ₹2.5 Lakhs.

### 3. Academic Requirements
Candidates must have passed their previous annual examinations.

No minimum marks are needed for basic post-matric schemes.

## Required Documents for Verification

Prepare clear digital scans of these documents before applying:

- **Aadhaar Card**: Must display your correct name spelling.
- **Income Proof**: Certificate issued by Mamlatdar or TDO.
- **Caste Certificate**: Digitally verified certificate for SC/ST/OBC.
- **Bonafide Certificate**: Issued by your current college head.
- **Fee Receipt**: Receipt of the current academic year.
- **Bank Passbook**: Copy showing active Aadhaar-linked account details.

## Step-by-Step Registration & Application Guide

Follow these steps to submit your application form:

### Step 1: Create a Citizen Profile
Visit digitalgujarat.gov.in and click on **Register**.

Provide your mobile number, email, and password.

Verify the registration using the mobile OTP code.

### Step 2: Complete the Profile
Login with your new credential details.

Enter your personal and address information.

Input your Aadhaar number and perform biometric verification.

### Step 3: Select Your Scholarship
Go to the **Services** section on your dashboard.

Click on **Scholarship Services** to view programs.

Choose the specific scheme matching your category.

### Step 4: Fill Academic Details
Enter your college name and course details.

Provide your previous marksheet percentages.

Input your active bank account number and IFSC code.

### Step 5: Upload Files & Submit
Upload all required document files under 200KB.

Review the completed form details very carefully.

Click **Submit** to forward form to your college desk.

## How to Check Portal Application Status

You can monitor the progress of your application online.

### Step 1: Portal Login
Access the login page on digitalgujarat.gov.in.

### Step 2: Navigate to Dashboard
Select the **Scholarship Application Status** menu option.

### Step 3: Read Verification Stages
Your dashboard displays the active stage of your file:

- **Pending at Principal**: College is verifying your records.
- **Pending at District Officer**: Under review by welfare officers.
- **Approved**: Verification is complete and treasury will pay.

## ⚠️ Common Reasons for Application Rejection

Avoid these errors to secure your scholarship amount:

- **Aadhaar NPCI Seeding Missing**: Bank must seed Aadhaar for DBT.
- **Invalid Income Proof Date**: Certificate must be currently valid.
- **Name Mismatch Errors**: Names on Aadhaar and marksheets must match.
