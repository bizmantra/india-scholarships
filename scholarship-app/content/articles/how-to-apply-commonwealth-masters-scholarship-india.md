The Commonwealth Master's Scholarship is a premier international award funding one-year taught Master's degrees at universities across the United Kingdom.

Funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) via the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC), the scholarship covers 100% of university tuition fees, return flights, monthly living stipends (~£1,712 outside London or ~£2,000 within London), and arrival allowances.

For a full breakdown of grant allowances and family support benefits, read our [Commonwealth Master's Scholarship scheme page](/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarship).

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: In India, you cannot apply to the UK CSC portal alone. The Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India acts as the National Nominating Agency. You must submit applications on both portals before their cut-offs to be eligible for nomination.

---

## The Dual-Portal Application Requirement

Commonwealth Master's applications in India follow a two-tier screening process:

1. **CSC Online Application System (OAS)**: The central UK portal (`cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply`) where you submit academic history, course choices, the Development Impact statement, and invite referees.
2. **Ministry of Education (MoE) India Portal**: The Indian nominating portal (via `education.gov.in` / Sakshat). The ministry shortlists candidates and nominates a limited number to the UK commission (39 candidates were nominated in the previous round).

Applying only to the CSC portal without an MoE submission results in automatic rejection because the UK commission only reviews officially nominated candidates.

## The 6 CSC Development Themes

Your UK Master's course and career goals must align with one of six development themes:

| CSC Priority Theme | Focus Areas | Example Eligible Courses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Science & Technology for Development** | Agriculture, engineering, biotechnology, clean energy | M.Sc. Renewable Energy, Agronomy, Data Science |
| **Improving Population Health, Health Systems & Capacity** | Public health, epidemiology, clinical research | M.Sc. Global Health, Health Economics |
| **Promoting Innovation & Entrepreneurship** | Sustainable business, technology transfer | M.Sc. Social Innovation, Tech Management |
| **Strengthening Peace, Security & Governance** | Law, public policy, human rights | Master of Public Policy (MPP), International Law |
| **Strengthening Resilience & Response to Crises** | Climate adaptation, disaster response | M.Sc. Environmental Change, Disaster Management |
| **Access, Inclusion & Opportunity** | Inclusive education, gender studies | M.A. Development Studies, Special Education |

## Crafting the Development Impact Statement

The Development Impact statement is the most critical part of your submission. It is structured into four specific sections:

1. **Relevance to Development**: Address a specific development challenge facing India. Cite data from NITI Aayog or UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
2. **Application of New Skills**: Explain how the chosen UK degree equips you with techniques or methodologies not readily available in Indian institutions.
3. **Target Beneficiaries and Outcomes**: Define who benefits from your work upon returning to India, detailing tangible social or economic outcomes.
4. **Measuring Impact Over Time**: Outline measurable milestones for 1–2 years after graduation and 5–10 years into your professional leadership career.

For practical structuring techniques, refer to our guide on [How to Write an SOP for Scholarships](/guides/how-to-write-sop-statement-of-purpose-scholarships).

> ⚠️ **Write it yourself**: Selectors look for a specific, personal plan. Generic or copied statements are easy to spot and rarely get shortlisted.

## Selecting Your 3 UK University Choices

On the CSC portal, you must list three separate UK Master's courses, ranked by preference:

- You do not need confirmed admission offers when submitting the scholarship form, but you must apply to those universities by December or January.
- Your essay must justify each selection by highlighting specific faculty, research facilities, or curriculum modules.
- If selected, funding is usually directed to your top-preference offer.

## Commonwealth Master's vs. Chevening Scholarship

Both are fully funded UK government awards, but they target different applicant profiles:

| Feature | Commonwealth Master's | Chevening Scholarship |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Funding Body** | FCDO via Commonwealth Commission | FCDO via Chevening Secretariat |
| **Nominating Agency** | Requires MoE India nomination | Direct application (no ministry involved) |
| **Work Experience** | Recommended, not mandatory | Minimum 2 years (2,800 hours) mandatory |
| **Core Emphasis** | Socioeconomic development in India | Leadership, networking, and diplomacy |
| **Application Deadline** | Closes **October 20, 2026** | Closes **October 6, 2026** |

If you have two or more years of full-time work experience, also review our [Chevening UK Scholarship Guide](/guides/chevening-uk-scholarship-2027-guide).

## Key Documents Required

- **Undergraduate Transcripts & Degree**: The CSC asks for the equivalent of a UK upper second-class (2:1) degree. The Ministry of Education sets its own minimum marks in each year's notice, so check the current one on `education.gov.in`.
- **Two Reference Letters**: Referees submit letters directly through secure links emailed by the CSC portal.
- **Valid Indian Passport**: Scanned copy of photo and address pages.
- **Admission Letters**: Upload conditional or unconditional offers if already received.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can final-year students apply?
Applicants must have completed their undergraduate degree and hold their degree certificate or provisional certificate by the MoE nomination date.

### Is IELTS required at the time of scholarship application?
The CSC does not mandate IELTS scores at the time of scholarship submission, but your chosen UK universities will require standard test scores for admission.

### Must scholars return to India after graduating?
Yes. Scholars must return to India after their studies. Switching to a Graduate or other UK visa to stay on is not allowed.
