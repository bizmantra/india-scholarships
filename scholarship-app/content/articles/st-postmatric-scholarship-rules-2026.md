## Overview of the ST Post-Matric Scholarship

The ST Post-Matric Scholarship is a Ministry of Tribal Affairs scheme that funds Scheduled Tribe students studying in Class 11 and above, including diploma, degree, and professional courses.

It reimburses tuition fees, pays a monthly maintenance allowance, and in many states adds a separate hostel or mess allowance for students staying away from home.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Use the [/tools/eligibility-checker](/tools/eligibility-checker) to see whether your state's tribal welfare department runs a supplementary scheme on top of the central benefit.

---

## Eligibility & Income Rules

| Criteria | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Category** | Scheduled Tribe (as per Central/State ST list) |
| **Income Ceiling** | ₹2.5 Lakh per annum (central); some states extend up to ₹6 Lakh |
| **Course Level** | Class 11 onwards — including ITI, Diploma, UG, PG, PhD |
| **Renewal** | Annual, subject to minimum attendance and passing marks |

---

## Hostel & Maintenance Allowance Explained

Many students confuse the maintenance allowance (a fixed monthly stipend for books, stationery, and personal expenses) with the hostel allowance (reimbursement of actual mess and lodging charges for students in a recognized hostel).

Both can be claimed together if you are a hosteller, but you'll need a warden certificate or hostel fee receipt in addition to your regular income and caste documents.

## State-Level ST Scholarship Variations

- **Maharashtra**: The TRTI Scholarship at [/scholarships/trti-scholarship-maharashtra-st](/scholarships/trti-scholarship-maharashtra-st) is run by the Tribal Research and Training Institute and includes a dedicated hostel component.
- **Tamil Nadu**: The combined SC/ST scheme at [/scholarships/tamil-nadu-post-matric-scholarship-for-scst](/scholarships/tamil-nadu-post-matric-scholarship-for-scst) adds book grants for professional courses.
- **Overseas Study**: Meritorious ST students pursuing masters or PhD abroad can apply for [/scholarships/ambedkar-overseas-vidya-nidhi-scst](/scholarships/ambedkar-overseas-vidya-nidhi-scst), which covers tuition, airfare, and living costs.

## Application Checklist

1. **Get your ST certificate verified** — some states require a fresh caste validity certificate for professional courses, not just the school-issued caste certificate.
2. **Match income certificate dates** to the current academic year's cutoff.
3. **Attach hostel proof** only if you're claiming the hostel/mess allowance separately.
4. **Track your disbursement** on PFMS once your application is verified by your institute.
