## Overview of UP Divyangjan Post-Matric Scholarship

The **UP Divyangjan Post-Matric Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement Scheme** is administered by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (*Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Vibhag*), Government of Uttar Pradesh.

This state welfare program provides 100% fee reimbursement and monthly maintenance allowances to students with disabilities pursuing Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Diploma, Graduation, Post-Graduation, or PhD degrees.

Students can view full scheme guidelines on the [UP Divyangjan Post-Matric Scholarship page](/scholarships/up-divyangjan-post-matric-scholarship).

---

## Scholarship Financial Benefits & Allowances

Eligible Divyangjan students receive direct financial aid across two components:

1. **Tuition Fee Reimbursement**: 100% of compulsory non-refundable fees charged by recognized colleges/universities (up to the government cap of ₹50,000 per year).
2. **Monthly Maintenance Allowance**: Ranging from ₹380 to ₹1,200 per month depending on course level and day-scholar/hosteller status.

All scholarship amounts are disbursed directly to Aadhaar-seeded student bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) monitored through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).

---

## Eligibility Criteria & Mandatory Requirements

To qualify for the UP Divyangjan Scholarship, applicants must satisfy the following conditions:

* **Disability Benchmark**: Must possess a certified disability of **40% or more**, verified by a competent government medical board or an active **UDID Card**.
* **Family Income Limit**: Total annual family income from all sources must not exceed **₹2,50,000 (2.5 Lakh per annum)**.
* **Residency Requirement**: Must be a bona fide permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh.
* **Institutional Enrollment**: Must be enrolled in a recognized government, government-aided, or private institution in Uttar Pradesh.

For national-level PwD opportunities, check the [Post-Matric Scholarship for Students with Disabilities (DEPwD)](/scholarships/post-matric-scholarship-for-students-with-disabilities-depwd).

## Step-by-Step Online Application Guide

Applications are processed online through the official state portal:

1. Visit the official Uttar Pradesh Scholarship portal (`scholarship.up.gov.in`).
2. Click on **Student Section** and select **New Registration**.
3. Choose **Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Vibhag (For PWD / Disabled Category)**.
4. Enter your personal details, Aadhaar number, UDID card number, and mobile number.
5. Log in using your registration number and password to complete the detailed application form.
6. Upload scanned copies of your disability certificate, income certificate, fee receipt, and previous mark sheet.
7. Verify all details and click **Submit Online**.
8. Print the submitted application form and present hard copies along with original documents to your college scholarship officer for online DIoS verification.

For complete state schemes, explore our [Uttar Pradesh State Hub](/scholarships-in/uttar-pradesh).

## Selection & Institutional Verification

Selection is eligibility-based for all qualifying PwD students in Uttar Pradesh:

* College scholarship committees verify student attendance, fee receipts, and original UDID cards.
* Verified applications are forwarded to the District Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Officer (DDSO) for financial approval and DBT disbursement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum disability percentage required?
Applicants must have a benchmark disability of at least 40% or more, supported by a valid UDID Card or government medical certificate.

### What is the family income limit for UP Divyangjan Scholarship?
The annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹2,50,000 (2.5 Lakh).

### Can students receiving central DEPwD scholarship apply for this state scheme?
No, students can only receive one government post-matric scholarship per academic session.
