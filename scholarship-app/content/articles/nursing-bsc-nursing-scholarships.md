## Nursing Students Are Covered Under Broader Technical Scholarships

BSc Nursing, GNM, and ANM students are frequently eligible for the same "professional/technical course" scholarships that cover engineering and pharmacy students — but nursing students often miss out simply because they don't realize they qualify.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: When searching state scholarship lists, look for "Professional and Technical Courses" wording — nursing is almost always included even if not named explicitly in the scholarship title.

## Watch Out for Bond-Linked Scholarships

Some private hospitals and nursing colleges offer scholarships tied to a mandatory service bond — you commit to working at the sponsoring hospital for a fixed period (often 2-3 years) after graduation. Read this clause carefully, since breaking the bond usually requires repaying the full scholarship amount with interest.

---

## Application Tips

1. **Confirm your course is council-registered** (State Nursing Council or Indian Nursing Council) — unregistered institutes disqualify you from most government schemes.
2. **Check for GNM/ANM-specific state stipends**, which are separate from BSc Nursing degree scholarships in several states.
3. **Track renewal deadlines closely** — nursing courses often have staggered semester schedules that don't align with the general academic calendar used by scholarship portals.
