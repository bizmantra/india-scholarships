## Arts & Humanities Students Have More Options Than They Realize

Humanities students often assume scholarships are concentrated in STEM and professional courses, but general post-matric and merit-based schemes apply equally to BA, MA, and research-track humanities students — the key difference is that fewer course-specific private CSR scholarships exist for this stream, so state and central schemes matter more.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: For Masters and PhD-level humanities research, check UGC-NET/JRF eligibility first — it's the single largest funding source for humanities scholars in India.

## UGC-NET/JRF for Humanities Research

Students pursuing an MA or MPhil/PhD in humanities and social science subjects can apply for the UGC-NET Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), which provides a monthly stipend plus a contingency/research grant for the duration of the fellowship — typically up to five years for PhD scholars.

---

## Application Tips

1. **Apply for state post-matric benefits first** — these have no stream restriction and form your baseline funding.
2. **Prepare a strong research proposal** if applying for postgraduate fellowships like JN Tata Endowment — humanities applications are evaluated heavily on the proposal's originality.
3. **Track UGC-NET exam cycles closely**, since JRF funding only starts from the cycle you clear, not retroactively.
