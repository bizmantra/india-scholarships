Clearing CSIR-UGC NET with a JRF award is the first step. The money starts only after you join a Ph.D. and your institution registers you for the fellowship.

This guide covers what happens after the result. For the stipend and eligibility, see the [CSIR-UGC NET JRF page](/scholarships/csir-ugc-net-junior-research-fellowship-jrf). Women doing a Ph.D. may also check the [UGC Savitribai Jyotirao Phule fellowship](/scholarships/ugc-savitribai-jyotirao-phule-single-girl-child-fellowship).

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Read your award letter line by line. It states who funds your fellowship and the time you have to join. Older UGC letters gave two or three years, so follow the one you were issued.

---

## Step 1: Get Your Award Letter

NTA issues JRF e-certificates and award letters on the NET portals (`csirnet.nta.ac.in` for CSIR-NET, `ugcnet.nta.ac.in` for UGC-NET). Save the PDF. You will need it for every later step.

Check:
- Which agency funds the award (CSIR or UGC).
- The deadline to register for a Ph.D.

---

## Step 2: Get Admitted to a Ph.D.

You need a full-time Ph.D. seat at a recognised university or institution. The fellowship begins from your joining date, subject to the rules in your award letter.

---

## Step 3: Submit the Joining Report

Your institute will ask for a joining report in the prescribed format. It usually needs:

- Your date of joining
- Signatures of your research guide and head of department
- Attestation by the head of the institution
- An undertaking that you are not receiving another fellowship or salary
- Copies of your award letter and master's marksheets

Ask your institute's research or fellowship cell for the exact format. They handle the next step.

---

## Step 4: Registration and Payment

| | CSIR-funded JRF | UGC-funded JRF |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who pays** | CSIR | UGC |
| **System** | CSIR's fellowship management system, run through your institute | UGC web portal run with Canara Bank |
| **Who registers you** | Your institute | Your institute links your record using maker/checker logins |

Both agencies revised the stipend in 2023: ₹37,000 a month for the first two years (JRF) and ₹42,000 a month after upgrade to SRF. The yearly contingency grant differs between CSIR and UGC, so read the figure in your own award letter.

For UGC awards, you do not need a Canara Bank account. The fellowship goes to the bank account you give, in your own name. Make sure the account is linked to Aadhaar and your name matches your award letter.

---

## What You Must Keep Filing

Fellowships continue only if the paperwork continues. Under UGC award letters, expect:

- A **continuation certificate** every three months
- Half-yearly progress reports
- A yearly statement of how you spent the contingency grant

Your institute also updates your record on the portal each month. If this is late, your payment can be late. Ask your institute's nodal officer for the schedule.

---

## JRF to SRF Upgrade

After two years of JRF, your work is assessed. If your progress is satisfactory, you move to Senior Research Fellowship (SRF) with a higher stipend. Your institute will tell you what report and presentation they need.

---

## Why Payments Get Delayed

1. **Joining report not submitted or not attested.** Nothing starts until the institute registers you.
2. **Aadhaar-bank mismatch.** Check your link with our [NPCI mapper guide](/guides/how-to-link-aadhaar-with-npci-mapper-for-dbt-scholarship).
3. **Name mismatch** between award letter, Aadhaar and bank account.
4. **Reports or continuation certificates pending** at your institute.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a JRF awardee take a job or another fellowship?
No. You sign an undertaking that you hold no other fellowship or salary.

### Is a Canara Bank account needed for a UGC fellowship?
No. Canara Bank runs the UGC portal. Payment goes to the account you give.

### What if the joining deadline in the award letter is missed?
Contact your institute and the funding agency at once. Do not assume you can extend it.

### How much is the stipend?
Check the current figure on the [JRF scholarship page](/scholarships/csir-ugc-net-junior-research-fellowship-jrf). Rates are revised from time to time, so confirm the latest circular.
