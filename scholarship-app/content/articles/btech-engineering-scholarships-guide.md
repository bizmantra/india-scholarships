## Three Types of Engineering Scholarships

Engineering scholarships in India generally fall into three categories, and understanding which bucket you fit determines which documents and cutoffs apply to you:

1. **Merit-based**: Tied to your entrance exam score (JEE Main/Advanced, BITSAT, state CETs).
2. **Need-based**: Depends on family income, regardless of merit rank.
3. **Category-based**: Reserved for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Minority students, often stackable with merit or need-based benefits.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Check whether your engineering scholarship is a one-time award or renewable annually — renewable awards require you to re-submit mark sheets every semester.

## How Fee Structures Work Across 4 Years

Most engineering scholarships disburse annually, not as a lump sum — you must reapply or submit a renewal form each year with your previous year's mark sheet. Some schemes reduce the benefit if your CGPA falls below a set threshold (commonly 6.5 or 7.0 on a 10-point scale).

Private colleges often require a separate "fee structure certificate" from the institute confirming the exact tuition amount, since government reimbursement schemes only cover fees up to the state-notified cap — anything above that is the student's responsibility.

---

## Renewal Checklist

1. **Maintain minimum attendance** (usually 75%) — many schemes cross-verify this with the institute.
2. **Submit updated income certificate** each year for need-based schemes, since certificates expire annually.
3. **Apply for renewal before the semester deadline** — late renewal applications are a leading cause of gap-year benefit loss.
