## Overview of UP Purva Sainik Kalyan Educational Scholarship

The **UP Purva Sainik Kalyan Educational Scholarship** is administered by Uttar Pradesh Purva Sainik Kalyan Nigam Limited (UPPSKNL) to reward academic excellence among children of ex-servicemen.

This welfare scheme grants direct cash awards to meritorious students completing Class 10 and Class 12 board examinations in Uttar Pradesh.

You can inspect the full scholarship guidelines on the [UP Purva Sainik Kalyan Educational Scholarship page](/scholarships/up-purva-sainik-kalyan-educational-scholarship).

---

## Award Amounts & Eligibility Cutoffs

The scholarship disburses financial aid based on board examination performance:

1. **Class 10 Board Award**: ₹40,000 total (payable in two equal installments of ₹20,000 each upon progression).
2. **Class 12 Board Award**: ₹30,000 lump-sum cash grant.

### Minimum Percentage Cutoffs:
* **CBSE / ICSE Boards**: Minimum 60% aggregate marks required.
* **UP State Board**: Minimum 50% aggregate marks required.

Family eligibility is restricted to a maximum of the two eldest dependent children of a registered ex-serviceman or Nigam civilian worker.

---

## Who is Eligible to Apply?

To qualify for financial aid under UPPSKNL guidelines, applicants must meet these criteria:

* **Dependency Status**: Must be the dependent son (up to 25 years of age) or unmarried daughter of a registered UP ex-serviceman or Nigam-paid employee.
* **Excluded Categories**: Children of senior Nigam officers (AGM rank and above) are not eligible.
* **Residency**: The ex-serviceman parent must be registered with Uttar Pradesh Purva Sainik Kalyan Nigam.

For general school grants, check the [UP Pre-Matric Scholarship](/scholarships/up-pre-matric-scholarship-general-sc-st).

## Offline Application Process & Submission Steps

The application process is handled through Nigam regional offices across Uttar Pradesh:

1. Visit the official UPPSKNL website (`upsainiknigam.com`) and download the Educational Scholarship SOP form.
2. Complete the form with parent ESM service number, unit details, and student board marks.
3. Attach attested copies of board mark sheets, ESM identity card, and admission proof for the higher class.
4. Submit the physical application packet to the Regional AGM (Operations) Office of UPPSKNL in your district.
5. Final merit lists are published on the official website, and funds are transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum age limit for dependent sons?
Dependent sons must be under 25 years of age at the time of application submission.

### How many children in a family can receive this award?
The scholarship is restricted to a maximum of the two eldest children of an eligible ex-serviceman or Nigam contract employee.

### Can students receiving state post-matric scholarship apply?
Yes, this merit reward from UPPSKNL is separate from general state post-matric tuition fee reimbursement.
