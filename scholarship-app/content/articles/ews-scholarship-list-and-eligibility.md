## Who Qualifies as EWS?

Economically Weaker Section (EWS) status applies to General category students whose family income is below the government-notified ceiling and who don't already benefit from SC/ST/OBC reservation.

The central EWS reservation ceiling is ₹8 Lakh per annum, but this figure is meant primarily for admission and job reservation — most EWS scholarship schemes (state and private) apply a much stricter income cutoff, often ₹2.5–4 Lakh.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Don't assume your ₹8L EWS certificate qualifies you for every scheme — check the scholarship-specific income limit using the [/tools/eligibility-checker](/tools/eligibility-checker).

## Government vs Private EWS Schemes

Government EWS schemes typically require a formal EWS certificate that also declares land and asset holdings — this is a stricter document than a plain income certificate, since it also checks agricultural land size and residential property.

Private CSR scholarships, on the other hand, usually accept a straightforward income certificate or even income tax returns/salary slips, without the asset-declaration requirement.

---

## Common Reasons EWS Applications Get Rejected

1. **Asset declaration mismatch**: Family owns agricultural land above the exempted limit, which disqualifies EWS status even if income is low.
2. **Certificate issued for the wrong purpose**: An EWS certificate issued for job reservation may not automatically be valid for a scholarship application — check the scheme's accepted document list.
3. **Overlapping category claims**: You cannot claim both OBC reservation benefits and EWS benefits on the same application.
