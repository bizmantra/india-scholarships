## Overview of European Fully Funded Scholarships

Studying in Europe offers world-class higher education and international career opportunities.

Germany and European Union member states fund thousands of international postgraduate students annually.

The DAAD scholarship program in Germany and the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master program cover complete educational costs.

These grants provide 100% tuition fee waivers alongside monthly stipends for living expenses.

Indian graduates from engineering, science, humanities, and management streams can apply for these awards.

## Understanding the DAAD Master Scholarship Scheme

DAAD is the German Academic Exchange Service funded by the German Federal Government.

It supports postgraduate study at public universities across Germany.

Most public universities in Germany charge zero tuition fees for master programs.

The DAAD scholarship provides a monthly stipend of €934 to cover health insurance and accommodation.

It also includes travel allowances, study subsidies, and family support where applicable.

---

## Key Eligibility Criteria for DAAD Grants

Applicants must hold a recognized 3-year or 4-year Bachelor degree from an accredited Indian university.

At least two years of professional work experience is required for specific development-related postgraduate courses (EPOS).

Academic excellence is evaluated based on undergraduate cumulative GPA or percentage.

Proof of English proficiency via IELTS (minimum 6.5 band) or TOEFL is mandatory.

Some German-taught programs require German language certificates (TestDaF or Goethe-Zertifikat).

---

## Understanding Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees (EMJM)

Erasmus Mundus is a prestigious international study program funded by the European Commission.

Each joint master degree program is delivered by a consortium of European universities.

Students study in at least two different European countries during their two-year master course.

The Erasmus Mundus scholarship covers full tuition, university enrollment fees, and mandatory insurance.

Scholarship recipients receive a monthly living allowance of €1,400 for up to 24 months.

---

## Step-by-Step Application Process for Erasmus Mundus

1. **Explore the Erasmus Mundus Catalogue**:
   Visit the official European Commission portal catalog. Search for master programs matching your academic background.

2. **Check Specific Entry Requirements**:
   Each consortium sets unique eligibility criteria, language prerequisites, and submission deadlines.

3. **Prepare Required Academic Documents**:
   Upload certified copies of degree marksheets, motivation letter, and recommendation letters before the deadline.

4. **Submit Application Directly to Consortium**:
   Applications open between October and January for the intake starting the following September.
