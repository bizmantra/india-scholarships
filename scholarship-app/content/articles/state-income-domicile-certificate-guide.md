## Why Income and Domicile Certificates Are Mandatory

Government scholarship schemes rely on official revenue documents to verify applicant eligibility.

The Income Certificate proves family earnings fall below the scheme's annual ceiling (for example, ≤ ₹2.5 Lakh/year).

The Domicile Certificate (Residency Proof) confirms permanent residence within the issuing state.

Without valid, unexpired revenue certificates, scholarship applications are rejected during institute verification.

## State Income Certificate Authority and Validity Rules

Income certificates must be issued by a competent government revenue authority.

Accepted issuing officers include Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Block Development Officer (BDO), or Revenue Circle Officer.

Salary slips issued by private employers or unverified notary affidavits are not valid proof for government scholarships.

Validity rules vary across Indian states:
- **Uttar Pradesh**: Valid for 3 years from date of issue via e-District portal.
- **Karnataka**: Valid for 5 years through Nadakacheri portal (Income & Caste combined certificate).
- **Jharkhand**: Valid for 1 financial year via JharSewa portal.
- **West Bengal**: Valid for 1 financial year issued by BDO/SDM via e-District portal.

---

## Domicile Certificate Rules for State Scholarships

State government scholarships are restricted to permanent residents (domiciles) of the state.

A Domicile Certificate is permanent unless your residential address changes.

Key documents required to apply for Domicile Certificate online:
- Proof of continuous residence (minimum 10 to 15 years in the state).
- School Leaving Certificate or continuous education certificates.
- Parents' land property deed or long-term house allotment receipt.

Students studying outside their home state can apply for home state scholarships if they possess a valid Domicile Certificate.

## How to Prevent Document Rejection on Scholarship Portals

Follow these practical guidelines to ensure your documents pass verification:

1. **Verify Name Spelling**:
   Ensure applicant name, father's name, and date of birth match your Aadhaar card and Class 10 certificate.

2. **Check Barcode and Digital Signature**:
   Modern certificates feature a QR code and digital signature. Unsigned physical drafts are rejected automatically.

3. **Check Annual Validity Period**:
   Re-issue your Income Certificate if it expires before the portal's application deadline.

4. **Verify Caste and Category Mapping**:
   Confirm that your caste category listed on the certificate matches the central or state reservation matrix.

5. **Maintain Certified Physical Copies**:
   Keep self-attested photocopies of all revenue certificates ready for college physical verification counters.
