> [!NOTE]
> **Key Takeaways for UP Scholarship 2026-27**:
> - The official Uttar Pradesh scholarship portal (`scholarship.up.gov.in`) is open for Pre-Matric (Classes 9-10) and Post-Matric (Classes 11-12, UG, PG, Diploma) fresh and renewal registrations.
> - Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) requires mandatory Aadhaar seeding with your active bank account linked to the NPCI mapper.
> - Final locked application form hard copies must be submitted to school/college scholarship officers within 10 days of online locking.

The Government of Uttar Pradesh provides annual educational fee reimbursement and maintenance allowances to millions of students under the Social Welfare Department, Minorities Welfare Department, and Backward Classes Welfare Department. Whether pursuing school education or professional degrees in engineering, medicine, management, or arts, understanding the registration procedure, district-level verification, and status tracking ensures timely disbursement.

## Schemes Covered Under UP Scholarship Portal

The portal categorizes schemes into three distinct levels:

| Scheme Category | Target Student Group | Maximum Income Ceiling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Matric Scholarship** | Students enrolled in Class 9 and Class 10 | ₹2,000,000 per annum |
| **Post-Matric Intermediate** | Students enrolled in Class 11 and Class 12 | ₹2,000,000 (Gen/OBC/Minority) / ₹2,500,000 (SC/ST) |
| **Post-Matric Other Than Inter (Dashmotar)** | Professional Degree, Diploma, UG, PG, PhD | ₹2,000,000 (Gen/OBC/Minority) / ₹2,500,000 (SC/ST) |

## Detailed Step-by-Step Application Procedure

To apply successfully without technical errors or form rejections, follow this procedural checklist:

### Step 1: Student Registration on Official Portal
1. Navigate to the official portal at `https://scholarship.up.gov.in`.
2. Click on the **Student** tab on the top menu and select **Registration**.
3. Select your category group (SC/ST/General, OBC, or Minority) and choose between **Fresh** or **Renewal**.
4. Enter your mobile number, Aadhaar number, high school roll number, and security captcha to generate your Registration Number and Password.

### Step 2: Form Completion & Document Upload
1. Return to **Student Login** and enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Password.
2. Fill in personal details, course enrollment date, fee structure, and previous year examination marksheets.
3. Upload passport-size photo (JPEG format, under 20 KB) and income/caste certificate serial numbers.

### Step 3: Form Checking & Final Locking
1. Click **Print Draft for Checking** and verify all details with your college scholarship department.
2. After confirming accuracy, click **Lock Final Application Form**. Once locked, no edits can be made.
3. Download and print the **Final Submission Form** after 3 working days.

### Step 4: Submission at Educational Institution
1. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents to the printed application form.
2. Submit the physical dossier to your institute scholarship counter.
3. Obtain a signed receipt/acknowledgment slip from your institute.

## Document Checklist for UP Scholarship

Ensure the following documents are ready before beginning registration:

- Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh
- Income Certificate issued by Tehsil (validity within 3 years)
- Caste Certificate (for SC, ST, OBC applicants)
- High School (Class 10) and Intermediate (Class 12) Marksheets
- College Admission Fee Receipt and Bonafide Certificate
- Bank Passbook Copy (showing IFSC Code and Account Number)
- Aadhaar Card (linked with mobile number for OTP authentication)

## How to Check UP Scholarship Status Online

Applicants can track real-time approval stages by checking their application status:

1. Visit `scholarship.up.gov.in` and log in to your candidate dashboard.
2. Click on **Check Current Status** under the Status menu.
3. Review the status indicators:
   - **Institute Verification Status**: Shows whether your college approved your form.
   - **District Scholarship Committee Status**: Indicates District Welfare Officer (DWO) clearance.
   - **PFMS Response**: Displays bank account validation and fund transfer status.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What to Do If College Name Is Not Listed on UP Portal?
Contact your college administration immediately to confirm if the institution has renewed its AISHE/DISE code affiliation on the UP scholarship master portal for the 2026–27 session.

### 2. Can Students Apply for Renewal After Failing One Subject?
Renewal applications require passing status in the preceding academic year. Students with backlog or carryover papers must check specific departmental guidelines before submitting renewal forms.

### 3. When Is Scholarship Money Credited to Bank Accounts?
Fund disbursement occurs in two main phases: First installment (maintenance allowance) is released between December and January, followed by fee reimbursement in February and March via Direct Benefit Transfer.
