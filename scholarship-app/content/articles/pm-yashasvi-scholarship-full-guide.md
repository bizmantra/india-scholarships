## What is PM Yashasvi?

PM Yashasvi — the Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India — is a central government scholarship for students from Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and Denotified, Nomadic, and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT), awarded through a competitive national entrance test rather than pure marks-based selection.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Unlike most state Post-Matric schemes, PM Yashasvi requires you to clear an entrance exam (YET) — start preparing at least a few months before the exam window opens.

---

## Entry Points and Exam Structure

| Entry Level | Eligible Class | Test Name |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Matric Entry** | After Class 8, entering Class 9 | Yashasvi Entrance Test (YET) |
| **Post-Matric Entry** | After Class 10, entering Class 11 | Yashasvi Entrance Test (YET) |

The test typically covers general aptitude, mathematics, language, and reasoning at a difficulty level appropriate to the entry class.

---

## Scholarship Amount

The benefit amount differs by entry level — Class 9 entrants receive a lower annual amount compared to Class 11 entrants, reflecting the increased cost of senior secondary education. Both entry levels include a component for tuition/school fees and a separate maintenance/stipend amount.

---

## How to Apply

1. **Register on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** during the announced application window, selecting the PM Yashasvi scheme.
2. **Download your admit card** for the Yashasvi Entrance Test once registration closes.
3. **Appear for YET** at your allotted exam center — results determine final selection, not just registration.
4. **Upload category and income certificates** after qualifying, for final verification before disbursement.
