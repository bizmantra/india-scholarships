## Step 1: Check Your PFMS Status Before Filing a Grievance

Before assuming your scholarship money is genuinely stuck, check your PFMS "Know Your Payment" status — a large share of "not received" complaints turn out to be failed transfers caused by an Aadhaar-bank seeding mismatch, not an actual processing delay.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: If PFMS shows "Rejected" or "Failure," your first step is re-seeding your Aadhaar with your bank via NPCI, not filing a grievance — the payment will simply fail again otherwise.

---

## Step 2: File on Your State Portal's Grievance Module First

Most state scholarship portals (NSP-affiliated or state-run) have a built-in grievance/helpdesk module. File your complaint here first — it's usually resolved faster since state officials directly handle disbursement verification.

| Stage | Where to File | Typical Resolution Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First attempt** | State scholarship portal grievance module | 2–4 weeks |
| **Escalation** | CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) | 4–8 weeks |
| **Final escalation** | State/Central Nodal Officer email (listed on NSP) | Case-by-case |

---

## Step 3: Escalate to CPGRAMS

If your state portal doesn't resolve the issue within a reasonable window, file a formal grievance on CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System), selecting the relevant ministry (Social Justice, Tribal Affairs, or Minority Affairs, depending on your scheme).

Attach your application ID, PFMS transaction reference, and a clear description of the issue — vague complaints without reference numbers take significantly longer to process.

---

## Common Reasons Payments Get Stuck

1. **Aadhaar not seeded (NPCI mapper)** — the single most common cause of failed DBT transfers.
2. **Institute verification pending** — your college hasn't confirmed your enrollment/attendance on the portal.
3. **Bank account frozen/closed** — inactive accounts fail silently during the transfer attempt.
