## Overview of Lucknow University Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship

The **Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship** is an internal financial assistance scheme operated by the Dean Students Welfare (DSW) office at the University of Lucknow to support financially weaker students.

Disbursed directly from the university's Student Welfare Fund (SWF), this scholarship provides financial aid to needy undergraduate and postgraduate students pursuing higher education at the campus.

Students can check full scheme requirements on the [Lucknow University Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship page](/scholarships/lucknow-university-chhatra-kalyan-scholarship).

---

## Financial Assistance & Eligibility Criteria

Selected students receive a direct financial grant of **₹15,000 per academic year** to cover tuition fees, examination charges, and essential educational expenses.

### Key Qualification Rules:
1. **Enrollment Status**: Must be a regularly enrolled student in an undergraduate or postgraduate degree program at Lucknow University campus or constituent departments.
2. **Family Income Ceiling**: Annual family income from all sources must not exceed **₹3,0,0,000 (₹3 Lakh)** per annum as certified by a competent authority.
3. **Academic Performance**: Must have scored at least **60% aggregate marks** in the previous annual or semester examination without any back papers.
4. **Attendance Threshold**: Minimum **75% attendance** in the preceding academic session certified by the concerned Head of Department (HOD).

For general degree fee reimbursement, see the [UP Post-Matric Scholarship (Other Than Intermediate)](/scholarships/up-post-matric-other-than-intermediate-general-sc-st).

## Step-by-Step Application Guide for DSW Lucknow University

Applications are processed offline through the DSW office following the conclusion of UG and PG admissions for the academic session:

1. Access the official Lucknow University website (`lkouniv.ac.in`) and download the Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship application form from the Student Welfare section, or obtain a printed copy directly from the DSW office.
2. Complete all required fields including student name, course name, roll number, department, and family annual income details.
3. Attach self-attested copies of admission fee receipts, previous mark sheets, income certificate, and departmental attendance certification.
4. Obtain and attach a notarized affidavit certifying that the applicant is not receiving any other government or private scholarship simultaneously.
5. Submit the completed application packet at the Office of the Dean Students Welfare, Lucknow University campus before the official deadline.

## Selection Committee Evaluation & Disbursement

Applications are evaluated by a university screening committee appointed by the Vice-Chancellor and chaired by the Dean Students Welfare:

* Applications undergo verification for income certificate validity, attendance percentage, and academic mark sheets.
* Shortlisted students may be called for document verification at the DSW campus office in Lucknow.
* Selected students (up to 50 beneficiaries per academic year) receive direct bank transfers into their bank accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can self-finance course students apply for Chhatra Kalyan Scholarship?
Yes, regular students enrolled in both regular and self-finance degree courses at Lucknow University campus are eligible to apply.

### Can students receive this scholarship alongside UP Post-Matric Scholarship?
No, applicants must submit an affidavit certifying that they are not receiving any other state or central government scholarship simultaneously.

### What is the annual family income limit?
The annual family income limit from all sources must not exceed ₹3,0,0,000 (3 Lakh per annum).
